\section{Data}

The unit of observation is a parent proposal: the set of filings that appear to represent one economic development. I construct historical parents from the Department of City Planning Housing Database and post-policy parents from Department of Buildings new-building filings. Historical MapPLUTO releases supply predetermined parcel characteristics, zoning, geography, and geometry. Filing dates, shared zoning lots, adjacent parcels, and common development-team fields help identify related filings. Housing Preservation and Development 485-x registrations provide an independent check on observed multi-filing configurations.

The main sample contains projects with at least six proposed residential units that could plausibly use the rental options of 485-x. It excludes confirmed condominiums and other developments that are clearly outside those options. Tenure is difficult to classify for recent projects because condominium offering plans can arrive years after the initial filing, so the condominium panel remains an audit rather than a main outcome.
