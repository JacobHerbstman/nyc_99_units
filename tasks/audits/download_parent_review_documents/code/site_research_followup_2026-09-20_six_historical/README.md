# Six historical cases: primary-source register, 2026-09-20

This is a bounded source-acquisition record for six historical parent IDs in
the Manhattan/Queens/Staten Island inventory. `sources.csv` gives literal URLs
and the exact source sections or PDF pages used in the companion report.
Existing original DOF event and parcel snapshots are already frozen under
`tasks/audits/audit_hdb_mappluto_condo_recovery/output/` and MapPLUTO releases
under `tasks/stage_mappluto_lots/output/`; they were read, not copied.

The six PDFs named in `sources.csv` are archived here as their original public
bytes. `sources.sha256` records their SHA-256 checksums. The Woodside agreement
exhibit on physical PDF page 7 was also visually checked: its red site boundary
encloses merged lots 8 and 9, with neighboring lots 23 and 39 outside it.
The South Street fact sheet was visually checked on physical PDF page 1; it
expressly describes a 41,479-square-foot site for the two towers and podium.
The city's September 2021 investigation report and December 2021 final work
plan resolve the fact sheet's inconsistent lot number: both name lot 2 as the
development site, and the later plan's Figure 2 maps its boundary apart from
the neighboring lot 1. The work plan's cover directly names DOB job
`M00558120-I1`.
The NY Courts PDF returned HTTP 403 to direct retrieval and remains cited by
URL only. Other HTML sources are likewise URL citations, not saved copies.
