# Six historical site conflicts: source follow-up (2026-09-20)

These six IDs were selected from the 48-case Manhattan/Queens/Staten Island
inventory and do not overlap the GO Broome, Sutphin, Flatbush, or concurrent
other-agent batches. The [source register](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_followup_2026-09-20_six_historical/README.md)
records exact first-party URLs and access limits. This is source gathering for
supervisor adjudication. It does not alter parent membership or production
areas. Counts and filing dates come from the frozen `parent_site_scope.csv`;
the DOB/DOF audit extracts cited below remain reproducible in the repository.

## `historical__420665845` and `historical__420665952`: 46-09 69th / 46-10 70th Street

The 295-unit West Building and 183-unit East Building were filed **June 1 and
June 14, 2019**, thirteen days apart. NYSDEC's [executed October 2019 BCP
agreement](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_followup_2026-09-20_six_historical/woodside_bcp_agreement_2019.pdf),
physical PDF pages **1, 3 and 7**, names both addresses under QB Development
Owner LLC. Page 3 states that the application covered old block 2432 lots
**8, 9, 21, 41, 44, and 50**, with a merger request to make only lots **8 and
9**; page 7 maps the boundary enclosing both lots and excluding neighboring
lots 23 and 39. The later [NYSDEC Site Management Plan](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_followup_2026-09-20_six_historical/woodside_smp_2021.pdf),
physical PDF page **14**, describes those two lots as one **71,862-square-foot**
site and identifies a 15-story mixed-use building and a 12-story residential
building as the redevelopment project. The 2020 [Remedial Action Work Plan](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_followup_2026-09-20_six_historical/woodside_rawp_2020.pdf)
gives the same two-building use before completion. A [2026 court opinion](https://www.nycourts.gov/reporter/pdfs/2026/2026_31891.pdf),
physical PDF page **3**, independently calls the two addresses West and East
buildings of one construction project, while distinguishing their building
locations.

DOF's saved transaction **89475/89489/89493** on March 17, 2020 confirms a
six-lot merger followed by division into 8 and 9, citing 2019 deeds (CRFNs
2019000117972/973) and a March 17, 2020 James C. Weed survey. The frozen
later DOF comparison gives ordinary lot 9 **29,504 square feet**; the East
building is indexed through condo billing lot 7502 at **42,397 square feet**,
which is not its underlying land parcel. The 71,862-square-foot combined site
is the documented common project scope, not either building's exclusive area.
The DEC report's land matches the observed two-building project and old-lot
list, so this is a strong **478-unit common economic-parent candidate** within
the cohort window. Before adopting 71,862 exactly, the supervisor should check
the recorded environmental easement or Weed survey against the DOB site plan
and old six-lot union; DEC area and current lot/condo area sums differ slightly.
Neither external project's preliminary unit estimate nor condo billing area
overrides frozen administrative units or measures independent ground.

## `historical__421133160` and `historical__421133179`: Vernon / 31st Drive

The two 51-unit DOB jobs were both filed **August 18, 2020**, on Queens block
502 lots 5 and 19. The official [June 14, 2021 City Record OER notice](https://a856-cityrecord.nyc.gov/RequestDetail/20210520005)
identifies **31-61 Vernon Boulevard** and **11-24 31st Drive** as separate VCP
sites **21CVCP037Q** and **21CVCP042Q** with the *same* applicant, 31-61 Vernon
Blvd LLC. Separate cleanup enrollment means neither site can be assigned to
the other building merely from ownership.

The saved original DOF transaction **160155**, effective August 15, 2024,
is more direct: its authority cites **both NB jobs 421133179 and 421133160**
and a Michael Khelawan survey dated August 24, 2023. It reapportions old lots
**5, 19, and 46** into new **5 and 19**, based on deed CRFN 2024000186685,
recorded July 22, 2024. Thus the two completed building parcels absorb land
from a third, formerly separate lot. The later DOF comparison reports **9,960
square feet for lot 5 and 9,968 for lot 19**, a 19,928-square-foot combined
recorded-lot benchmark. Production still carries 6,174 and 5,400, so the
reference map misses land in old lot 46; the 20v4 MapPLUTO archive records
old lot 46 at **6,800 square feet**. Summing those old attributes gives
18,374, not 19,928, a material discrepancy requiring the 2023 survey and
complete 2020-era map before an archival-area replacement.

Same-day filings, same cleanup applicant, adjacent sites, and a DOF action
explicitly naming both jobs support a **102-unit economic-parent join
candidate**. The distinction is that the OER notices define separate
environmental sites and the DOF action reallocates land; neither alone states
whether the jobs share common development ground or gives the old-lot-46
allocation. Obtain the Khelawan survey or deed schedule and both DOB ZD1/site
plans before adopting the 19,928 current-area sum or an archival crosswalk.
Preserve the separate 2020 DOB job identities and filing dates.

## `historical__M00558120`: 265 South Street

The frozen parent is a 1,313-unit September 7, 2021 filing on Manhattan block
247 lot 2. Production still carries **31,341 square feet**; the audit's two-old-
lot envelope is **145,031**. The newly archived NYC OER [September 2021
Remedial Investigation Report](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_followup_2026-09-20_six_historical/south_street_rir_2021.pdf),
physical PDF page **8**, explicitly calls the then-current site **block 247
lot 2, 31,341 square feet**. It says the applicant had filed with DOF to move
the lot line, enlarging lot 2 to the proposed **41,479-square-foot** project
site while reducing adjacent lot 1. The December 2021 [final Remedial Action
Work Plan](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_followup_2026-09-20_six_historical/south_street_final_rawp.pdf)
directly names **DOB job M00558120-I1** on its cover (physical PDF page **1**).
Its executive summary (page **8**, repeated at **19**) says DOF **approved**
the reconfiguration, adding approximately **10,138 square feet** to lot 2:
31,341 + 10,138 = **41,479**. It assigns the 67- and 73-story towers to the
five-floor common podium on this lot. Its Figure 2 (physical PDF page **58**)
plots the magenta site boundary on **lot 2**, separately from the north-adjacent
**lot 1**; the proposed building footprint falls within that site boundary.
The figure is a visual site plan, not a metes-and-bounds survey, but it directly
links the enlarged parcel to the proposed building. The saved later DOF
comparison independently reports **41,479 square feet for lot 2**. DOF
transactions **93872/93910** merged old lots 1+2 and reapportioned them in
October 2021; the latter cites Robert J. Fehringer's April 1, 2021 survey and
CRFNs 2014000308098/2021000101748.

NYC OER's [December 2021 fact sheet](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_followup_2026-09-20_six_historical/south_street_oer_fact_sheet_2021.pdf),
physical PDF page **1**, also states 41,479 square feet and the two towers,
but calls the site **lot 1**. The investigation report contains one reversed
lot-1/lot-2 parenthetical on page 8 even as its heading and other passages
consistently identify the site as lot 2. These appear to be labeling errors:
the final work plan's DOB-job cover, approved-lot-change text, mapped Figure 2,
and the later DOF area all independently identify the project ground as lot 2.
Thus **41,479 square feet is supported for adoption as the observed filing's
ground site**. The old 145,031-square-foot two-lot envelope includes adjacent
Land's End II land and must not be assigned to this filing. This adjudication
does not treat lot 1's separate buildings or wider zoning rights as part of
the observed ground.

## `historical__420666283`: 72-01 Queens Boulevard (SOLA)

The frozen 364-unit job was filed **September 27, 2019** on Queens block 1352
lot **125**. Production uses its stale old recorded **22,800 square feet**;
the DOF audit identifies three reference lots 71/121/125 at **59,736** total.
DOF transactions **89039/89040**, effective February 18, 2020, merged these
three into lot 71 then reapportioned into lots **71 and 125**, citing three
October 2018 deeds and a Richard Tom survey dated December 16, 2019.
The later DOF comparison reports lot 125 at **58,036 square feet**, while its
mapped geometry measures approximately 56,953; use the recorded field rather
than map polygon area. [DOF's own 2025–26 assessment](https://a836-pts-access.nyc.gov/care/datalets/datalet.aspx?LMparent=20&UseSearch=no&jur=65&mode=asmt_tent_2026&pin=4013520125&taxyr=2027)
confirms lot 125's 58,036 square feet and identifies one 12-story building at
the 72-01 address. The [developer's SOLA project page](https://sbdg1oak.com/portfolio/sola/)
identifies **one 364-unit, 12-story mixed-use building** at 72-01 Queens
Boulevard, with retail, matching the administrative job. Its 380,000-square-
foot figure is building floor area, not site ground.

The 58,036-square-foot successor lot is the best bounded later-land candidate;
it almost exhausts the three old lot attributes but leaves a 1,700-square-foot
difference and the DOF action retains lot 71. The primary project page does
not show whether any retained lot-71 ground is accessory to SOLA. The Tom
survey or DOB ZD1 is needed to establish the entire development boundary and
exact archival-parcel allocation. This is a clear **production-area problem**,
but not yet a documented final historical 58,036-square-foot adoption.
