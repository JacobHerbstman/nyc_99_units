# Review estimation parents, companions and refilings

The current complete-characteristic, A/B, 50-plus-unit casebook contains 92 multi-constituent parents: 40 historical and 52 post-policy, with 226 source filing rows. Historical outcomes and descriptions use the inactive-inclusive HDB 23Q4 archive. Post-period outcomes use HDB 25Q4 with DOB fallback. July 2026 DOB fields provide separately labeled retrospective comparison evidence.

`review_parents.R` assembles each case from saved membership, accepted links and administrative sources. Original documentary findings remain in `code/parent_evidence.csv`, with URLs and limitations. `parent_review_coverage.csv` maps those records through filing IDs and compares additive filing sets with the dated September 22 baseline. Changed sets retain prior findings as partial evidence. Parents without an individual record remain explicitly unreviewed. An unchanged filing set does not establish complete site coverage or legal wage treatment. Adopted production decisions belong to `parent_opportunities_manual`.

`screen_neighbors.R` screens all 905 weighting parents against positive-unit new-building records within 200 metres and 365 days of a member, retaining same-lot matches regardless of distance. Historical candidates come from HDB 23Q4; post-period candidates come from the saved July 2026 DOB extract. Members already inside a parent are excluded. The screen finds 1,397 parent–filing pairs, including 1,277 without an individual finding in the original neighbor evidence table. Six anchor filings lack coordinates. Source coverage, missing coordinates and the date window limit this screen; different owner labels do not prove separate beneficial ownership.

`review_refilings.R` checks the production replacement decisions. Historical automatic matches use archived BIN, lot and exact address, withdrawn status and a later nonwithdrawn application within the parent window. The archive does not provide withdrawal dates. Post-period matches use the observed DOB withdrawal date and owner/applicant agreement. Manual archived alternatives retain their recorded evidence basis. The output includes the full linking universe, including years outside the canonical 2019–2022 historical panel: 97 historical and 12 post-period predecessor filings, carrying 3,063 nonadditive source units. Canonical-period totals are reported in the pre-policy source audit.

`trace_companions.R` checks the seven originally accepted companions against the period-appropriate linkage fields and geometry coverage, and verifies their shared-parent membership. The Third Avenue PNG renders October 2025 RAWP page 77. The original detailed investigation remains in `report/ten_case_deep_review.md`.

Public PDFs are acquired by `download_parent_review_documents`; analysis builds perform no live searches. After preparing upstream data through root Make, run Make from `code/`. The local Makefile lists actual products and input symlinks. SaveData produces data reports as side effects; reports and execution logs are not targets. The root logbook build prepares the upstream data and compiles the research record.

## DOB-only unit comparison, September 10

`compare_unit_sources.R` compares current constituent counts with the actual July 2026 DOB NOW I1 extract. It does not use the historical membership field `dob_i1_units`, which currently repeats HDB units and therefore is not independent DOB evidence. The comparison holds retained filings, parent membership, original/refiling dates and exposure classifications fixed. It is an audit, not a production source switch or a reconstructed initial-submission history.

`output/dob_unit_filings.csv` has each retained filing, current/HDB/DOB counts, source update dates and coverage. `output/dob_unit_parents.csv` gives corresponding totals and exact repeated-99 patterns. Strict DOB totals are missing when any constituent lacks DOB coverage. Columns named `available_dob_*` explicitly retain current units for unmatched filings: that historical sensitivity is not DOB-only. Standard data reports accompany both outputs.

Relative to production revision 5fd812c, all 973 post-period A/B constituents match DOB. Counts differ for 31 filings in 30 of 876 parents. Exact-99 constituents fall from 93 to 90; the 50-plus filing denominator rises from 414 to 415, so the exact-99 share falls from 22.46% to 21.69%. Nine exact 99+99 parents remain nine, while exact 99+99+99 parents fall from two to one. Total units rise from 59,467 to 59,676 (+209). The comparison includes reversing the externally documented Turnbull increase: 228 returns to the filed 91. Third Avenue becomes 98+97+99=294; Myrtle's affected filing changes from 99 to 79; Boyland becomes 86+29=115; Grand Concourse becomes 59+56=115.

Only 783 of 1,671 historical A/B constituents match the saved DOB NOW extract; 888 lack that direct match, chiefly legacy job identifiers. Within available matches, 34 counts change across 32 parents. The explicitly mixed-source sensitivity changes historical exact-99 filings from six to five, but this is not a complete historical DOB-only comparison. Three Smith Street job records each carry 899 in the DOB extract versus HDB constituent counts of 513, 333 and 70. Their raw sum would be 2,697 instead of 916. The repeated total needs application/physical-building review before using it additively. A complete switch also requires checking minimum-unit sample entry and exit and any unit-dependent classification; this audit freezes the current sample to isolate measurement differences.

The earlier Turnbull correction conflated a financing program with filing-reported dwelling units. The saved I1 and nine supporting records all report 91; external evidence of 228 apartments should remain separate from the filed count. The comparative audit quantifies the proposed reversal and wider source change without silently choosing a different historical coverage rule.
