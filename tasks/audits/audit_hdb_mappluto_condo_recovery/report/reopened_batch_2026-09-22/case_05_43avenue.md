## Disposition for the review and manual record

1. **Boundary correction:** mark the 2025 parent's reference/development BBL as `4004280001` and area as `33950`, supported by the 2021 Finance map and 2024 Finance administrative sale row. Mark the historical parent's original BBL and area identically, supported by the 2008 map and archived 18v2 area. There is no area correction to the current panel; its 33,950 already matches.
2. **Filing relationship:** record `420666363` as a prior same-site *sequential opportunity* for `Q01263805`, not `reviewed_retained_bbls`, an ancillary filing, a separate physical site, or a constituent to combine. Preserve 2019-10-18 and 2025-08-12 filing dates and the adopted 83 and 387 HDB units. The gap is well beyond the parent-link 365-day window. Treat the 2025-07-23 status date as later evidence, not as an instruction to remove the old proposal from the historical source vintage.
3. **Review implementation:** the current `parent_site_document_review.csv` schema can document the parcel but **cannot by itself clear this flag**: `audit_parent_site_scope.R` includes `shared_land` in `unresolved` after applying a documented boundary. A narrow reviewed sequential-opportunity field or exception for this exact pair is needed if the audit is meant to pass validated reuse. That change should preserve both parents and should not generalize to all same-BBL collisions.

Suggested boundary document row fields for `post_policy__Q01263805-I1` (subject to the table's normal validation): `decision=complete_reference_parcel_supported`; `reference_bbls=4004280001`; `reference_recorded_area_sqft=33950`; `document_id=40042820210609123051`; `document_date=2021-06-09`; `source_pages=1`; `source_url=https://propertyinformationportal.nyc.gov/pdf/home/index/map_library/40042820210609123051`; `development_bbls=4004280001`; `development_area_sqft=33950`; `area_basis=DOF_tax_lot_recorded_area`; `reviewed_filing_bbls=4004280001`; `reviewed_hdb_bbls=4004280001`; `reference_vintage=23v3_1`; `reviewed_component_jobs=Q01263805`; `reviewed_retained_bbls` and `reviewed_ancillary_jobs` empty. The reason should name the historical job as an earlier whole-lot alternative and point to ACRIS deed `2024030300037001`, without claiming that a 2024 deed predates the 2023 reference. An analogous historical row would use map `40042820081207105706`, `reference_vintage=18v2_1`, and component `420666363`. The separate pair-level review should identify `historical__420666363` and `post_policy__Q01263805-I1` with relation `sequential_same_site_opportunities`, preserving each original filing date and vintage-specific unit source.
