The Motto

Address: 2455 Third Avenue & 227 East 134th Street, Bronx, New York
Size: 264 Residential Units
Type: Rental, Parking, Retail


Situated in the vibrant Mott Haven neighborhood of the South Bronx, The Motto is a recently completed 264-unit multifamily tower. This building features two connected24-story towers, which incorporates 14,000 square feet of parking, over 3,000 square feet of retail space, and an unparalleled amenity package. Conveniently located just four blocks away from the 4, 5, and 6 subway lines and one block from Bruckner Boulevard, a burgeoning corridor of retail, bars, and restaurants, the property offers easy access to transportation and local hotspots. Residents can explore nearby attractions such as BronxArtSpace, and the South Bronx Farmers Market, while also enjoying a short drive to iconic landmarks like Yankee Stadium and the Bronx Botanical Garden.
