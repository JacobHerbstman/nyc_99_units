# Bedford Square: four Flatbush historical filings, 2026-09-20

This is a bounded source packet for `historical__B00698117`, `historical__B00698115`, `historical__B00698118`, and `historical__B00722009`. Administrative filing dates, units, and older lot areas come from the frozen `output/parent_site_scope.csv` and `output/parent_site_other_filings.csv`. The original primary documents, exact public URLs, acquisition date, and SHA-256 values are recorded in [sources.csv](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_batch10_2026-09-20_flatbush/sources.csv). The supervisor adjudicates the audit source tables and production changes separately.

## Finding and source crosswalk

The supervisor accepts the four filings as one **877-unit economic development**. They are separate DOB jobs, and this finding does not determine a single legal wage-assessment unit. The filings arrived within 31 days: 2201 Beverley Road (`B00698115`, 296 units) and 2363 Bedford Avenue (`B00698118`, 132) on March 21, 2022; 2366 Bedford Avenue (`B00698117`, 354) on March 22; and 158 Lott Street (`B00722009`, 95) on April 21. The October 2023 [NYSDEC remedial investigation report](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_batch10_2026-09-20_flatbush/bedford_beverly_rir_2023-10-26.pdf), physical PDF pages 13–14, identifies one former Sears redevelopment site spanning Brooklyn block 5133 old lot 14 and block 5135 old lot 53, and says the project is expected to construct **four new seven-story residential buildings**. The earlier [January 2023 BCP application](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_batch10_2026-09-20_flatbush/bedford_beverly_bcp_application_2023-01-19.pdf), physical PDF page 33, instead described a *conceptual* two-tower design; it does not describe the later four-building plan. The developer's [Bedford Square page](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_batch10_2026-09-20_flatbush/bedford_square_the_district_2026-09-20.html) names the four observed addresses as Buildings A–D under one project. The 2024 [NYSDEC BCA Amendment No. 2](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_batch10_2026-09-20_flatbush/bedford_beverly_bca_2024-02-29.Amendment_No2_Add_Applicant.pdf), physical PDF page 1, says the newly added titleholding entities will become individual building owners after remediation while original Bedford Beverly Acquisitions LLC and Manhattan Management Realty LLC stay in the brownfield agreement. That distinguishes later titleholding from the joint development.

The supervisor inspected the original easement and **adopted the two west-building physical grounds** (lots 46 and 44) in the audit. The east-building allocations are also fully identified at DOF's administrative precision: the dated maps and job-naming transaction show a complete two-lot partition, and current DOF and DCP agree on each area. Under the audit's established **earlier-reference convention**, the combined parent has **177,520.79 sf**: the distinct old east lot 53 at 60,650 sf once, plus surveyed west lots 44 and 46 at 43,413.10 and 73,457.69 sf. A *later successor-lot sum* is **177,596.79 sf** using the current east lots' 25,479 and 35,247 sf instead. The 76-sf east difference is a measurement-vintage distinction, not a missing parcel or an unresolved scope question. Both totals exclude the retained Sears lot 14 and count each piece of ground once. If only administrative current lot areas are used, DOF/DCP reports 177,609 sf for the four successor lots, **12.21 sf higher** than the mixed surveyed/current total, entirely from the two west lots.

## West block: surveyed building lots and excluded Sears land

The [recorded environmental easement](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_batch10_2026-09-20_flatbush/bedford_beverly_recorded_ee_2024-12-20.pdf), a December 2024 image scan in the NYSDEC repository, is unusually useful because its Schedule A reproduces the **deed descriptions** for all three successor tax lots on block 5133. Physical PDF page 3 names 2201 Beverly Road / lot 44 / Bedford Beverly A LLC, 2366 Bedford Avenue / lot 46 / Bedford Beverly B LLC, and retained Sears parcel lot 14 / Bedford Beverly 2360 LLC. Its deed-description text on pages 13–14 gives 73,457.69 sf for lot 46, 43,413.10 sf for lot 44, and **53,905.83 sf for retained lot 14**. The latter belongs to the landmark Sears property, not one of the four new housing filings. The easement itself covers only **1.87 acres of controlled property** (PDF p. 4), with 69,335.45- and 12,328.00-sf restricted portions described ahead of the deed descriptions (PDF p. 13). Those restricted portions are environmental controls, not the building-ground denominators.

The [DOF old map](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_batch10_2026-09-20_flatbush/dof_map_30513320130301164034.pdf) was effective March 1, 2013–November 8, 2023 and shows one old lot 14. The [successor map](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_batch10_2026-09-20_flatbush/dof_map_30513320231114144956.pdf), effective November 14, 2023, draws separate lots 14, 44, and 46. Official DOF transaction **99953**, effective November 8, 2023, calls lots 44 and 46 new and lot 14 affected; its authority names lot-14 deed CRFN **2023000289030** (recorded November 6, 2023) and Paul D. Fisher's September 8, 2023 survey. ACRIS's public master index resolves the deed to document ID **2023110300122001**. The later transaction **99999** is explicitly a correction to lot 46's *length*, not another land transfer.

The 21v1 MapPLUTO record and the January 2023 brownfield application report **126,877 sf** and about **2.913 acres** for old lot 14. Those figures are incompatible with the three current recorded deed descriptions, which sum to **170,776.62 sf**, and the same pre/post DOF map perimeter. The December 2024 deed-description total is **43,899.62 sf more** than the old 21v1 record. Current DOF/DCP administrative areas for lots 14, 44, 46 are 53,902 + 43,414 + 73,469 = **170,785 sf**, only **8.38 sf** above the surveyed deed-description total. The older west-block area is therefore not suitable for the four-building denominator. The DEC application/RIR's roughly 187,527-sf two-block cleanup-site area inherited the older lot figures and also includes the retained Sears property; it cannot replace the successor building-lot union.

## East block: complete successor partition with a 76-sf revision

The [DOF old map](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_batch10_2026-09-20_flatbush/dof_map_30513520081206135345.pdf), effective December 6, 2008–June 7, 2024, shows one lot 53 on block 5135. The [successor map](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_batch10_2026-09-20_flatbush/dof_map_30513520240607105717.pdf), effective June 7, 2024, shows exactly two disjoint successors: new lot 29 fronting Lott Street and retained lot 53 fronting Bedford Avenue. Other lots on the block are outside this subdivision. DOF transaction **147303** calls lot 29 new and lot 53 affected. Its recorded authority explicitly names DOB new-building jobs **B00722009 and B00698118**, the April 17, 2024 deed CRFN **2024000098255**, and Arkadiusz Jusiega's November 9, 2023 survey. ACRIS's public master index resolves the deed to document ID **2024041600140001**.

The [DOF FY2026–27 assessment response](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_batch10_2026-09-20_flatbush/dof_assessments_five_lots_2026-09-20.json) assigns 25,479 sf to lot 29 and 35,247 sf to lot 53 in both years' final-period records; [DCP PLUTO 26v2](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_batch10_2026-09-20_flatbush/pluto_current_five_lots_2026-09-20.json) independently repeats both values and identifies both as Bedford Beverly 2359 LLC property. The successor sum is **60,726 sf**, **76 sf greater** than old lot 53's 21v1 recorded 60,650 sf. The maps and job-naming transaction settle which job gets which physical lot and exclude outside parcels. The subdivision deed or survey might explain the small measurement change or provide greater precision, but is not required to establish scope. The 158 Lott DOB description, “ONE ZONING LOT WITH (2) TAX LOTS,” does not itself put both tax lots beneath that one filing.

## Scope and precision

The west deed-description schedule is decisive for the two west building lots and retained Sears parcel. The east pair has a complete DOF map partition, an explicit two-job crosswalk, and agreeing DOF/DCP administrative areas. CRFN 2024000098255 and the cited Jusiega survey were not independently retrieved; they could refine the east areas but are not needed to identify the two building lots or the complete old-lot union. The environmental cleanup boundary and two-tax-lot zoning statement do not replace the specific successor-lot allocation.
