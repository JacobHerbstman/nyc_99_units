# 4388 Broadway / 673 West 187 Street: earlier twelve-unit filing

Parent: `post_policy__M01393124-I1`; review date: 2026-09-22. This is a research recommendation, not an implemented adjudication.

## Decision

The flagged `historical__123766433` is a **prior, apparently unbuilt development proposal on the same ground as the later 72-unit building**, not evidence of a second contemporary building or extra land. Its old lot-35 plan should remain visible as a 2019 historical filing, with its original filing and permit dates, but should not by itself make the 2026 parent a shared-land case. The earlier permit's definitive current DOB disposition and the 2026 zoning diagrams were inaccessible in this review. Therefore the stronger claim that DOB formally superseded or withdrew job `123766433` remains unverified. A source-qualified audit exception for the earlier plan is appropriate; a generic rule that treats all old permits as dead is not.

The current candidate ground is unchanged: Manhattan block 2170 lots 3 and 35, BBLs `1021700003;1021700035`, recorded MapPLUTO area **7,691 + 2,373 = 10,064 square feet**. The two saved DOB NOW initial filings propose **99 + 72 = 171 units**, and the lot polygons have a common boundary segment (the source WKT shares vertices `(1002756.376,250529.031)` and `(1002766.886,250547.981)` in State Plane feet). Both list Westorchard Management / Steven Westreich as owner and Leandro Nils Dickson as architect, and both describe sixteen-story new buildings. These are substantial economic-parent links, though not proof of one legal zoning lot or one wage-assessment unit. Do not add the earlier 12 units to this 2026 parent.

1. Add a reviewed earlier-plan exception for parent `post_policy__M01393124-I1` and job `123766433` to the documentary review source. The decision should read `earlier_unbuilt_plan_on_redeveloped_lot` (or equivalent), reference BBL `1021700035`, and cite the 23Q4/25Q4 snapshots, 2023 vacant-lot sale listing, 23v3.1 MapPLUTO, and both DOB NOW job IDs. Keep `123766433` as a historical observation with its 2019 filing date and 2022 permit; do not reclassify it as nonresidential or call it withdrawn without DOB evidence.
2. In the site-scope audit, allow a **documented earlier-plan job** to clear `site_scope_conflict` without labeling it `documented_separate_site` (it is the *same* lot) or `documented_ancillary_filing` (it is residential). This is a local, explicit adjudication of overlap, not a blanket exemption for old permitted filings. Then `other_direct_filings` and `shared_land` should fall to zero for this parent, and the site status can return to `Supported by available parcel and filing checks` provided other flags remain false.
3. No change to parent membership, 171 proposed units, BBLs, recorded land **10,064**, `ResidFAR=3.44`, or current `BldgArea=0` / prior-site-use assignment is warranted from this evidence. Keep any broad FAR `6.5` as the existing MapPLUTO facility FAR field, not a verified residential FAR. Neither the old 9,156 marketing estimate nor new-building 80,920/56,615.9 construction figures are pre-existing floor. No parcel allocation beyond lot 3 = 7,691, lot 35 = 2,373 is supported.

Remaining documentary question: retrieve the BIS job disposition/CO history for `123766433` and DOB NOW ZD1 plans for `M01393124-I1` and `M01417673-I1` if a claim about legal zoning lot, unused transferable rights, or definitive supersession is needed. The economic-parent recommendation does not require that stronger claim.
