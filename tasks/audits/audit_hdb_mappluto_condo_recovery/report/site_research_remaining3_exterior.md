# 355 Exterior Street: job-specific ground allocation

## Finding for supervisor review

The new primary record resolves the earlier ambiguity. DOB job `210182069` is a single connected 710-unit building drawn across **Parcel 3 (old lots 46/47, 355 Exterior)** and **Parcel 2 (old lot 100, 399 Exterior)**. It does not occupy the four State waterfront/rail parcels in the broader zoning lot, the mapped park, or the adjacent old-lot-38 building. The best exact administrative area for the combined building site is the later merged tax lot 46, **120,272 square feet**. The job's **189,018-square-foot zoning lot** is broader and should not be used as physical development ground.

For production, the supervisor adopted one explicit homogeneous-feature group over reference old lots **38, 46, 47, and 100**: reference administrative area **225,839 square feet**, development administrative area **120,272 square feet**, and excluded pre-existing floor area **25,962 square feet**. The retained pre-existing floor area is therefore **31,850 square feet**, giving built FAR `31,850 / 120,272 = 0.264816`. All four reference lots have residential FAR **3.44** and broad FAR **6.5**, so no invented within-group area allocation is needed for either weighted zoning measure. The four reference lots and their mixed use/zoning labels remain explicit. This note does not alter any central decision or production file.

## Exact-job evidence

The official DOB job feed identifies document 01 of job `210182069` at 355 Exterior Street, old lot 46: filed **2020-10-13**, approved **2021-10-22**, 710 proposed units, 43 stories, 726,179 square feet of construction floor area, and 565,497 square feet of zoning floor area. Those are the frozen filing facts; later DOB activity does not change the parent count.

The original DOB ZD1 (`ES615641380`) is dated **2020-11-30**, revised and signed **2021-10-07**, and was scanned **2021-10-19**. Physical p. **2** is decisive. Its site plan draws one connected proposed base and two towers across the parcel line between **Parcel 3** and **Parcel 2**. The same sheet labels the adjacent two-story building **OUT**, labels the mapped park **OUT**, and puts the Oak Point Link/State strip beyond the shore public walkway. Physical p. **4** reports the same 710 dwelling units and 565,497 square feet as the filing feed. This is a direct job-to-ground assignment, rather than an inference from address or ownership.

The ZD1 title block lists zoning-lot lots `100, 46, 47, 146, 108, 120, 25`, and p. **2** reports total zoning-lot area **189,018 square feet**. The recorded zoning declaration (`2021100100618001`), physical pp. **3–8**, confirms that lots 46/47 are the 355 parcel, lot 100 is the 399 parcel, and lots 120, 146, 108, and 25 are State parcels. Physical p. **5** reports those four State parcels as **68,722 square feet** in total. The broad 189,018 figure therefore measures zoning rights, not the ground assigned to the building.

The earlier DOB PD1 (`ES079163428`) was signed **2020-12-17** and scanned **2021-01-21**. It identifies the exact job and plots the full zoning lot, but it does not allocate the proposed building. It is corroborating chronology only; the later ZD1 supplies the allocation.

## Development and ownership crosswalk

The January 2021 DEC remedial action work plan was prepared after the DOB filing but before approval. Physical p. **10** defines the 355 subject property as old lots 46/47, approximately 62,500 square feet, with a 31,850-square-foot warehouse. Physical pp. **15–16** say the development plan for that subject property was to demolish the structure and construct a mixed-use building, landscaped area, access roads, and sidewalks. Figure 2 on physical p. **81** maps that cleanup boundary. This establishes the 355 half of the development, but the later job-specific ZD1 shows that the approved building also crosses onto the separately remediated 399 ground.

The July 2021 exchange deeds place both building parcels in the same ownership proportions before DOB approval:

- ACRIS `2021091800077018`, physical pp. **2–4**, conveys a narrow part of old lot 38 from 325 Exterior to 355 Exterior, to be part of lot 46. The legal courses yield about **3,284.88 square feet** when the final short curve is represented by its chord.
- ACRIS `2021091800077026`, physical pp. **3–7**, conveys lots 46/47, including that old-lot-38 tract, to 355 Exterior Street Associates LLC (51.64%) and 399 Exterior Street LLC (48.36%) as tenants in common.
- ACRIS `2021091800077027`, physical pp. **3–6**, conveys old lot 100 to the same tenants in common at the same 51.64/48.36 shares.

The deeds are dated **2021-07-29/30** and were recorded **2021-09-24**, before the ZD1 revision and the **2021-10-22** job approval. They independently support one economic building site across old 355 and 399, while leaving the State parcels outside that ownership.

The DOF tax map effective **2023-01-04** depicts old lots 47 and 100 absorbed into enlarged lot 46 while old lot 38 remains separate. Current official PLUTO version 26v2 reports that new lot 46 as **120,272 square feet**, with application BBL `2023490038` and application date **2023-01-27**. This is later administrative evidence and is used only for the exact post-reconfiguration ground area; it does not revise the 710-unit filing date or count.

The lot-100 deed conveys the whole old tax lot and repeats Parcel 2's legal boundary; it does not describe a partial-lot conveyance. Its archived pre-merger administrative area is **58,165 square feet**. That supports whole-lot-100 inclusion in the approved building site, but it does not turn 58,165 into a surveyed contribution to the later merged lot. The successor total would leave **62,107 square feet** for Parcel 3 if the old lot-100 administrative figure carried through unchanged, yet neither the approved plan nor a recorded survey provides the internal successor-area split. The production disposition therefore preserves all four source lots as one group and does not assign the 62,107 residual to old lot 46 or any other single reference parcel.

## Prior floor area and FAR

The 20v5 archive records these pre-filing inputs: old lot 38, 105,168 square feet / 25,962 building square feet / R7-2 residential FAR 3.44; old lot 46, 29,200 / **31,850** / R7-2 3.44; old lot 47, 33,306 / **0** / R7-2 3.44; and old lot 100, 58,165 / **0** / C4-4 residential FAR 3.44. Their reference administrative areas sum to **225,839 square feet**, and their building areas sum to **57,812 square feet**. DEC's 355 work plan independently identifies the 31,850-square-foot warehouse on lots 46/47. The July 2021 DEC decision for the separate 399 cleanup site, physical p. **7**, describes old lot 100 as a parking lot, consistent with zero building floor area in 20v5.

ZD1 physical p. **2** places the two-story old-lot-38 building wholly in the area marked `OUT`; only the narrow upland-connection tract enters the project parcel. The retained building is therefore the group's **25,962-square-foot excluded floor area**. Subtracting it from the four-lot reference total gives **57,812 - 25,962 = 31,850 square feet** of pre-existing floor on the development ground. Every contributing 20v5 parcel has residential FAR **3.44** and broad FAR **6.5**, so both group-weighted zoning measures remain those common values despite the unresolved internal successor-area split.

The recorded 20v5 areas for old lots 46, 47, and 100 sum to 120,671 square feet. Adding the approximately 3,284.88-square-foot old-lot-38 tract gives 123,955.88, which is 3,683.88 above the later 120,272-square-foot lot. This mismatch is evidence that the archived administrative areas and the deed-course estimate are not exact additive survey measures through the reconfiguration. The area proposal rests on the job-specific Parcel 2/Parcel 3 assignment plus the later DOF merged-lot record, not on forcing those earlier figures to sum or proportionally scaling them.

## Remaining limit

The ZD1 establishes the physical allocation clearly enough to exclude the State parcels and include both old 355 and old 399. It does not print a subtotal for Parcels 2 and 3. The 120,272-square-foot value comes from the later merged DOF lot that maps the same combined site. A survey certificate stating the Parcel 2/Parcel 3 subtotal would provide surveyed precision, but no contrary retained-building or separate-phase evidence appears in the reviewed originals.
