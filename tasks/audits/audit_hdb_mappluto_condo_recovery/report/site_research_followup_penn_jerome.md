## `historical__M00600672`: 335 Eighth Avenue, Penn South

The frozen **188-unit** DOB filing is dated **2021-10-27** and indexed to Manhattan block 751 lot 20, though the archival 21v1 parcel match instead carries the **393,100-sf Penn South campus lot 1**. The original [recorded memorandum of ground lease](https://a836-acris.nyc.gov/DS/DocumentSearch/DocumentImageView?doc_id=2022122800734016), document ID `2022122800734016`, CRFN `2023000006118`, is dated **2022-12-22** and recorded **2023-01-06**. Its cover, p. **1**, indexes **block 751 lot 20, entire lot**, with the address variant **341 Eighth Avenue**. On p. **3**, Penn South's Mutual Redevelopment Houses, Inc. demises to **335 Eighth Avenue Owner LLC** the 341 Eighth premises as described in Exhibit A. This owner name, ground-lease role, lot, and location tie the recorded parcel to the 335 Eighth development despite the street-number variant. Penn South's [2023 annual report](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_followup_2026-09-20_four_large/eighth_avenue_penn_south_2023.pdf), pp. **5, 14**, independently calls the project its 335 Eighth ground-lease site.

Exhibit A, p. **7**, starts at the **northwest corner of Eighth Avenue and West 26th Street**, runs west **247 ft**, north **163 ft**, east **247 ft**, and south **163 ft**. It expressly describes a **portion** of Penn South's earlier land. The leased rectangle encloses **247 × 163 = 40,261 square feet**, exactly the recorded lot-20 area and the parcel on the [DOF split map](https://propertyinformationportal.nyc.gov/pdf/home/index/map_library/10075120220914110818), p. **1**, effective **2022-09-14**. The prior [DOF map](https://propertyinformationportal.nyc.gov/pdf/home/index/map_library/10075120081209150257), p. **1**, effective **2008-12-09**, shows the older unsplit campus lot. Thus the **leased development ground is 40,261 sf**, with the rest of Penn South's land retained outside this demise. The full unrecorded lease could contain other use rights, and the recorded memorandum does not establish the legal wage-assessment unit or the precise 2021 zoning lot; it does establish the economic ground actually leased for the building.

## `historical__210181747`: 2520 Jerome Avenue, St. James Terrace

The frozen **102-unit** DOB filing is dated **2020-07-09**, indexed to Bronx block 3190 future lot 10. The frozen 20v3 old-lot-1 reference area is **46,563 sf**, covering both church and future building land; the [cohort DOF tax map](https://propertyinformationportal.nyc.gov/pdf/home/index/map_library/20319020081205133939), p. **1**, still shows that one old lot. The original [recorded deed](https://a836-acris.nyc.gov/DS/DocumentSearch/DocumentImageView?doc_id=2021070800201002), document ID `2021070800201002`, CRFN `2021000293554`, is dated **2021-06-30** and recorded **2021-07-29**. Its cover, p. **1**, indexes **block 3190 lot 10, entire lot, 2520 Jerome Avenue**. On p. **3**, St. James Church conveys **2518–2520 Jerome, lot 10** to **SJF Property Steward LLC**, expressly a portion of the church's formerly larger land and referencing its confirmatory deed recorded as CRFN `2020000211532` on **2020-07-27**. The deed therefore documents a separate conveyance out of retained church ground, although its execution followed the DOB filing.

Deed Schedule A, p. **6**, begins on the **east side of Jerome Avenue, 170.25 ft north of East 190th Street** and bounds an eight-course parcel: north **126 ft**, east **157.01**, south **82.63**, west **43.56**, south **16.84**, west **8**, south **26.56**, and west **105.94 ft** back to Jerome. Its printed interior and exterior angles specify a stepped southern edge rather than a 126-by-157-foot rectangle. Reconstructing those directions and applying the shoelace formula gives **17,774.63 sf**; the rounded courses close within **0.01 ft** of their starting point. This corroborates **17,775 sf** for new lot 10 on the [DOF split map](https://propertyinformationportal.nyc.gov/pdf/home/index/map_library/20319020210421110212), p. **1**, effective **2021-04-21**, and in later DOF/PLUTO records. The retained church lot 1 is separately recorded at **29,681 sf** in the later administrative snapshot; it is **not** part of the deed's conveyed ground.

The original [recorded memorandum of lease](https://a836-acris.nyc.gov/DS/DocumentSearch/DocumentImageView?doc_id=2021070800201004), document ID `2021070800201004`, CRFN `2021000293556`, is also dated **2021-06-30** and recorded **2021-07-29**. Its cover, p. **1**, indexes **block 3190 lot 10, entire lot**. On p. **3**, SJF leases 2518–2520 Jerome, lot 10, to **Concern St. James HDFC as nominee for CHP St. James LLC**; p. **9** repeats the deed's exact Schedule A eight-course boundary and its light-and-air-easement caveat. The transferred **and leased housing ground** therefore has the same **17,775-sf** boundary. The light-and-air easement, plus separate 2021 easement and declaration (`2021070800201003`, `2021070800201007`) indexed to **both** lot 10 and retained lot 1, shows shared rights must be distinguished from conveyed/leased ground. The Shaw survey underlying the 2021 split is dated **2020-06-11**, before the DOB filing, but the survey image and DOB zoning/site drawing have not been obtained. The original recorded deed and lease establish the completed housing parcel and church retention; they do not by themselves prove the exact July 2020 zoning-lot schedule or legal wage-assessment unit.

This geometry is a check on the deed description, not a replacement for the administrative recorded area.
