# 27-01 Jackson Avenue: historical parent `historical__420665667`

## Decision

**Resolve the shared-filing flag as an earlier, unbuilt plan for the same physical development site.** DOB job `421067767` proposed a 15-story, 91-unit building at 27-19 43 Avenue on Queens block 432 lot 21. The later DOB job `420665667` proposed the 27-story, 164-unit building at 27-01 Jackson Avenue on that same lot. The [City Planning Commission's August 2018 disposition report, C 180385 PPQ, pp. 2–3](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/planning/download/pdf/about/cpc/180385.pdf) states directly that the 9,195-square-foot lot 21 was a *vacant corner lot* after its service station was demolished in 2015 and that its owners had filed permits for a 15-story mixed-use building in 2015. The record therefore identifies the older filing as a plan for this lot, not a second completed building or a second physical site. The 18v2Beta PLUTO snapshot independently records zero existing building area and zero built FAR on lot 21 before the later 2019 filing.

Keep the archived 23Q4 parent at **164 residential units and one component**. Retain physical candidate/reference land **BBL `4004320021`, 9,195 square feet**, with **0 square feet of existing floor area and built FAR 0** in 18v2Beta. Record job `421067767` as an earlier alternative plan for that same site, not an additional 91 units, component, or independent parent. Clear this parent's `shared_land`/`unresolved` finding arising solely from that older job. The formal DOB withdrawal flag is not needed for this conclusion: the dated City Planning account of the vacant site and the historical PLUTO record establish that the 15-story building was not standing at the reference vintage. The current DOB feed has a 2024 special-action code `W` for the older job, but its `withdrawal_flag` is `0`; I do not interpret that combination as proof of formal withdrawal in 2018 or at 23Q4.

**Keep the zoning lot separate from the ground site.** The same City Planning report (pp. 2, 5–6) documents 15,754 square feet on City-owned bridge-approach lots 18 and 29, to be merged with lot 21 into a **24,949-square-foot zoning lot** for a transfer of **66,368 square feet of development rights**. It says the new building's footprint would be limited to the private development site, lot 21 (p. 5). The [related bulk permit C 180382 ZSQ, pp. 1, 5](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/planning/download/pdf/about/cpc/180382.pdf) and [parking permit C 180383 ZSQ, pp. 1, 5](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/planning/download/pdf/about/cpc/180383.pdf) likewise name lots 18, 21, and 29. This is a documented zoning-rights relationship, not reason to add bridge land to the physical parent weighting area. If a separate zoning-site variable is maintained, its documented lots and area are `4004320018;4004320021;4004320029` and 24,949 square feet. No production land decision was edited here.
