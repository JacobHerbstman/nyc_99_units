Areas are square feet. Previous values are the actual production values saved before this correction, not the audit's larger candidate envelopes.

**Eagle/West.** The [2022 confirmatory deed](https://a836-acris.nyc.gov/DS/DocumentSearch/DocumentImageView?doc_id=2022012501219001), pp. 2–3 and 6–7, identifies lot 21 and prints 106,018 square feet based on an October 5, 2018 survey. The filing-era map, two HPD premises schedules, and all three DOB lot identifiers connect the 745-unit phase to this ground. Old lots 2 and 21 contribute 54,503 and 51,515 square feet after subtracting retained lots 2 and 3. Those contributions sum exactly to the survey area. Both earlier parcels have zero floor area and residential FAR 6.02. DOF transaction 83289 is the first step of the same December 2018 reapportionment. The separately flagged transactions 84181, 86750 and 87034 correct lot labels and paper-street treatment; the dated 2019–2020 maps preserve the same outer boundary. The 2022 confirmatory deed includes that boundary explicitly. No extra ground or backdated removal of street rights is inferred. [Evidence and pages](site_research_remaining3_eagle_followup.md).

**Exterior.** The [original DOB ZD1](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_remaining3_2026-09-21_exterior/exterior_dob_zd1_ES615641380.pdf), p. 2, draws one connected building across old lots 46/47 and 100, plus a strip from old lot 38. The neighboring old-lot-38 building is outside the development. The July 2021 deeds put both building parcels in the same tenancy. DOF's successor lot records the combined ground at 120,272 square feet. The 189,018-square-foot zoning lot also includes State land. The earlier area attributes do not add exactly through the reconfiguration, so the adopted value is the later administrative measurement of the verified combined boundary. It is not represented as surveyed precision at filing. [Evidence and pages](site_research_remaining3_exterior.md).

Exterior's four contributing earlier lots share residential FAR 3.44 and broad FAR 6.5. A single documented combined area therefore suffices to retain these FARs without inventing an internal land split. The producer preserves all four lot identities and mixed zoning/use. It subtracts the 25,962 square feet of building floor on excluded old-lot-38 land from the four parcels' 57,812, leaving 31,850. Existing density is 31,850 / 120,272 = 0.264816. The grouped-source rule checks identical FARs; heterogeneous FARs still require separate documented allocations.

**Story.** The [2020 declaration](https://a836-acris.nyc.gov/DS/DocumentSearch/DocumentImageView?doc_id=2020102600056002), pp. 3–4, 9 and 11, describes lots 1 and 10 and assigns them to 1580 Story and the same owner. Their administrative areas sum to 151,992 + 51,918 = 203,910; the printed legal courses yield 203,912.11, a 2.11-square-foot drafting difference. Retained cooperative land and southern lots 35/36 are separate. The shared parking obligation does not add those southern parcels to the building ground. The earlier lot's zero floor area, parking use, and residential FAR 2.43 remain. Later records establish the observed development boundary without backdating title to June 2019.
