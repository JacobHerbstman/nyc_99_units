\noindent Ground is measured in square feet. Previous ground is the production
value before this correction, rather than a provisional audit envelope.

Eagle's 2022 confirmatory deed prints 106,018 square feet from an October 2018
survey, preceding the three March 2019 filings. The filing-era map, HPD
premises schedules, and DOB lot identifiers connect the three-building phase
to that boundary. Earlier lots 2 and 21 contribute 54,503 and 51,515 square
feet after subtracting the two retained parcels. The sum matches the survey
exactly. Both source parcels have zero existing floor area and residential
FAR 6.02. DOF merger 83289 is the first step of this same reapportionment,
followed that day by the creation of the retained parcels. The separately
flagged March and October 2019 lot-face and paper-street changes preserve the
outer perimeter in the dated maps. The 2022 deed confirms that same boundary;
this does not backdate extinguishment of street rights.

Exterior's exact-job DOB zoning drawing shows one connected building across
old lots 46/47 and 100, with an acquired strip from old lot 38. The neighboring
old-lot-38 building lies outside the development. The later merged tax lot
measures 120,272 square feet; its broader 189,018-square-foot zoning lot
includes State land. Earlier area attributes do not reconcile exactly through
the reconfiguration, so the adopted value is the administrative area of the
verified combined boundary. It is not certified surveyed precision at filing.
All four contributing earlier lots share residential FAR 3.44 and broad FAR
6.5. The code checks that equality and preserves the four lot identities and
mixed zoning/use without inventing an internal area split. Excluding the
neighboring building's 25,962 square feet leaves 31,850 of earlier floor area,
for existing FAR 0.264816.

Story's recorded declaration assigns lots 1 and 10 to 1580 Story under the
same owner. Their administrative areas sum to 203,910 square feet; printed
legal courses yield 203,912.11. Retained cooperative parcels and the separate
southern property are outside this physical allocation. The shared parking
agreement does not add the southern ground. Earlier zero floor area, parking
use, and residential FAR 2.43 are preserved. The later declaration establishes
the observed development ground without establishing successor ownership at
the June 2019 filing.
