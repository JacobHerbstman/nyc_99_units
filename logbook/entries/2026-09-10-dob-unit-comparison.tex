\section*{September 10, 2026: Compare filing-reported units before changing sources}
\textit{Agent-drafted audit following Jacob's request to use DOB filing counts.}

The preceding entry's Turnbull correction used a financing program to replace a filing-reported count. That was the wrong basis for a filing-size measure. The saved DOB initial application and nine supporting filings all report 91 dwelling units; HDC's 228-apartment program is separate evidence. The comparison below includes reversing this override and switching the other retained buildings to the saved DOB initial-application records. It does not change production counts yet, because the historical source coverage and repeated project totals require resolution.

For the current A/B sample in January 2025--July 8, 2026, all 973 constituents match the saved DOB NOW extract. Switching their units changes 31 constituents and 30 of 876 parent totals. Exact-99 filings fall from 93 to 90, while the number with at least 50 units rises from 414 to 415. The corresponding exact-99 share falls from 22.46\% to 21.69\%. All nine exact 99+99 parents remain; exact 99+99+99 parents fall from two to one. Third Avenue becomes 98+97+99=294, while Myrtle's affected filing becomes 79 rather than 99. Total proposed units rise from 59,467 to 59,676, a net increase of 209 including Turnbull's reduction of 137.

Historical coverage is incomplete. Only 783 of 1,671 A/B constituents in 2019--2022 match the saved DOB NOW extract. Replacing those available counts changes 34 constituents across 32 parents and reduces exact-99 filings from six to five. The other 888 constituents retain their current units in this explicitly mixed-source sensitivity. The historical membership variable named \path{dob_i1_units} currently repeats HDB; this audit instead joins the actual DOB source and leaves unmatched DOB counts missing. That existing label must not be interpreted as independent validation.

A mechanical historical replacement also creates a substantive problem at Smith Street: three linked jobs each report 899 units in DOB, giving a raw sum of 2,697 versus HDB's 513+333+70=916. Their application and physical-building relationships need review before treating those totals as additive. The present calculation holds membership, dates and classifications fixed; it does not rerun selection or reconstruct each application's original design. An I1 record observed in July 2026 can contain amendments made after its initial submission.

The additional case review narrows several earlier concerns. At 769 East 232nd, the DOB record reports 22,680 construction square feet and 55 units; contemporary filing coverage describes a floor schedule summing to 55. Wharf's saved filing explicitly reports 483 units and forty stories, versus a nine-story 104-unit proposal reported in 2022. Grand Concourse's older 56-unit, eleven-story HDB record differs from its later 59-unit, twelve-story DOB record. These are source and timing questions, not grounds to substitute external project totals. The across-street Boone boundary remains a separate membership question.
