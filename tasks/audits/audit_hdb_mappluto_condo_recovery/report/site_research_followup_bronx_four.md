## Casa Celina executed-lease area resolution

The original [ACRIS memorandum of lease](https://a836-acris.nyc.gov/DS/DocumentSearch/DocumentImageView?doc_id=2021063001174002) is archived as `casa_memorandum_lease_2021063001174002.pdf` and hashed in the source manifest. Its cover (physical p. **1**) gives document date **2021-06-24**, recording date **2021-07-08**, document ID `2021063001174002`, and CRFN **2021000258843**. The memorandum (physical pp. **3–4**) names NYCHA as landlord and Casa Celina LLC and its HDFC as tenant; section 4 says **the leased premises are described on Exhibit A**. Thus p. **9** is an executed-record description of the demised ground, stronger than a proposed board-resolution area or later budget summary. Exhibit A also subjects the parcel to separate light-and-air and drainage easements; it does not equate the ground parcel with the entire zoning-rights area.

I independently read Exhibit A p. **9** and checked its courses. Start at the Watson/Thieriot corner as `(0,0)`; proceed west **84.00 ft** to `(-84,0)`, south **158.00 ft** to `(-84,-158)`, east **76.23 ft** to `(-7.77,-158)`, north along a **right-hand radius-45-ft curve** through **81°06′51″** to `(0,-100)` approximately, then north **100 ft** to start. Replacing the curve temporarily with its chord gives a five-vertex polygon of **13,046.67 sf** by the shoelace formula. The curve removes the circular segment `45²/2 × (θ − sin θ) = 433.05758 sf`, with `θ = 81°06′51″` in radians. The enclosed ground is therefore **12,613.61242 sf**, within the printed-course rounding of both the board's **approximately 12,614 sf** and the DCP/DOF lot-39 recorded area of **12,613 sf**. The curve's computed **63.7069-ft** length matches the printed **63.70 ft** to rounding. The geometry supports use of **12,613 sf as the administrative building-ground area**, on later lot 39 carved from frozen 19v2 block 3730 lot 1 (recorded **344,900 sf**). It does not support assigning the retained NYCHA campus to the building.

The [NYCHA 2025 operating-plan narrative](https://home4.nyc.gov/assets/nycha/downloads/pdf/annual-plan/2025/2025-2029-annual-plan-operating-plan-narrative.pdf), physical p. **20**, says NYCHA leased **approximately 9,400 sf on old lot 1** in 2021 for Casa Celina; exact wording is preserved as a labeled [web text capture](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_followup_2026-09-20_bronx_four/nycha_2025_plan_web_extract.txt). The same number appears in NYCHA's [2023 budget book, physical p. 35](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/nycha/downloads/pdf/annual-plan/2023/fy-2023-2027-annual-plan-budget-book.pdf) and [2026 budget book, physical p. 33](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/nycha/downloads/pdf/annual-plan/2026/2026-2030-annual-plan-budget-book.pdf). These are later summary statements without a boundary or measurement method. The executed lease's legal description resolves the **demised ground**, but **why the budget books report 9,400 sf remains unexplained**. That number cannot be called a building footprint on this evidence. No administrative unit count or original filing date is changed here.
