# Four large Brooklyn historical parcel allocations

This is a source acquisition and case review, not an audit-table edit. Source
PDFs, raw API responses, URLs, page numbers, and SHA-256 checks are in the
[September 20 acquisition](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_followup_2026-09-20_brooklyn_large/README.md).
Housing Database units and filing dates remain the adopted administrative
values. Whole rail yards, campuses, zoning lots, and legal agreements are not
automatically the physical ground of one filing.

The ACRIS legal/master indexes were screened for each block. For 698 Atlantic,
the 2018 cooperation agreement on lots 19/28/35 identifies a wider B5–B7 rail
yard transaction, but not a B5-specific legal plan. ACRIS labels De Kalb
`2023071200021002` and `2023071200021004` DEVR in its master index, but the
originals are a development-agreement memorandum and a temporary
construction-license memorandum, respectively. Their actual text governs the
different lot-15 development and lots-1-and-15 protection scopes.
