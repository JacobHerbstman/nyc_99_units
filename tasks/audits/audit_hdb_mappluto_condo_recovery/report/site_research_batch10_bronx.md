## `historical__220700873`: 1580 Story Avenue, 562 units

The original [deed, ACRIS document `2019012100162007`](https://a836-acris.nyc.gov/DS/DocumentSearch/DocumentImageView?doc_id=2019012100162007), was executed **2019-01-15** and recorded **2019-01-22** as CRFN `2019000024735`, before the **2019-06-05** DOB filing. Its cover, physical p. **1**, indexes the **entire** Bronx block 3627 lot 1 and names Soundview Estates LLC as seller and Boynton Properties LLC as buyer. Schedule A on physical pp. **5–6** starts at Boynton/Story, follows the northern Story frontage with two indentations, proceeds down Morrison Avenue, then returns along a **950-foot** southern edge. Reconstructing the 12 printed straight courses gives **276,732.6 square feet** (coordinate closure under 0.01 foot after printed rounding). This agrees with the 19v2 lot-1 recorded **276,732 square feet** and with the contemporaneous northern-strip parcel shown on the [DOF map effective 2018-12-19](https://propertyinformationportal.nyc.gov/pdf/home/index/map_library/20362720181219165934), p. **1**. It contradicts the frozen 18v2beta lot-1 recorded **553,463 square feet**, which is about twice the deed area and cannot be used as this filing's measured ground.

The deed establishes the whole **pre-split ownership parcel**, not the 562-unit building allocation. DOF transaction `87593`, effective **2019-11-12**, later split lot 1 into residual 1 and lots 10/25/45; the [post-split map](https://propertyinformationportal.nyc.gov/pdf/home/index/map_library/20362720191112150303), p. **1**, and present PLUTO **151,992-square-foot** residual lot 1 cannot be backdated to June. The ACRIS index lists a **2019-09-24** zoning-lot declaration, document `2019092700468002`, indexed on lots 1 and 35, and a **2020-10-13** declaration, `2020102600056002`, indexed on lots 1, 10, and 35. These postdate the filing, and index membership does not disclose the boundary or rights in their unseen exhibits. **Proposed disposition: remain open** on the exact 562-building ground. Obtain the approved 2019 DOB site/zoning drawing for job `220700873`, or the dated original zoning declaration/survey with an explicit crosswalk to that job. The later withdrawn 562-unit `X00756455-I1` is not a proved replacement on this evidence.

## `historical__210182069`: 355 Exterior Street, 710 units

The [2020-02-04 DEC Brownfield Cleanup Program application](https://extapps.dec.ny.gov/data/DecDocs/C203129/Application.BCP.C203129.2020-02-04.Complete_BCP_Application.pdf), physical p. **20**, and its boundary Figure 2, physical p. **32** (drawing dated **2019-09-20**), identify applicant/owner **355 Exterior Street Associates LLC** and environmental site `C203129` as block 2349 old lots **46 and 47**, approximately **1.43 acres**. The [2020-04-30 executed agreement](https://extapps.dec.ny.gov/data/DecDocs/C203129/Agreement.BCP.C203129.2020-04-30.Executed_BCA.pdf), p. **3**, repeats those lots and area. The newly archived [March 2021 DEC decision](https://extapps.dec.ny.gov/data/DecDocs/C203129/Decision%20Document.BCP.C203129.2021-03-22.DD.pdf), physical p. **7**, describes a proposed mixed-use building, open land, access roads, and sidewalks on that cleanup site. This is strong contemporary **physical environmental-site** evidence associated with the named 355 owner, but it does not display the **2020-10-13 DOB 710-unit site plan**.

The neighboring [July 2021 DEC decision for 399 Exterior, site `C203139`](https://extapps.dec.ny.gov/data/DecDocs/C203139/Decision%20Document.BCP.C203139.2021-07-17.DD.pdf), physical p. **7**, describes a **separate 1.34-acre** property, corresponding to old lot 100 in the [2020-02-27 DOF cohort map](https://propertyinformationportal.nyc.gov/pdf/home/index/map_library/20234920200227090957), p. **1**. Later amendments add different prospective purchasers to the two DEC agreements: **355 Exterior Development LLC** to `C203129` ([January 2025 application](https://extapps.dec.ny.gov/data/DecDocs/C203129/Agreement.BCP.C203129.2025-01-07.Amendment_No1_355_Exterior_Street_Site.pdf), physical pp. **2–3**) and **399 Exterior Partners LLC** to `C203139` ([January 2025 application](https://extapps.dec.ny.gov/data/DecDocs/C203139/Application.BCP.C203139.2025-01-08.Amendment_No1_New_Applicant.pdf), physical p. **3**). These later amendments concern applicants, not an established 2020 DOB ground boundary.

The ACRIS index supplies the original 2019 lot-46/47 deed `2019022800494001` and a separate old-lot-100 deed `2019030400262001`. The now-archived [zoning-lot declaration `2021100100618001`](https://a836-acris.nyc.gov/DS/DocumentSearch/DocumentImageView?doc_id=2021100100618001) was dated **2021-09-30** and recorded **2021-10-07**, about a year after the filing. Its physical p. **3** names **355 Exterior Street Associates LLC** as applicant for present or future permits and defines a much broader zoning lot comprising **block 2349 lots 46, 47, 100, 120, 146, 108, and 25**. The N.B./Alt. permit-number fields on that page are **blank**; the declaration does not name DOB job `210182069` or 710 units. Physical pp. **3–5** give separate legal descriptions; p. **4** describes a portion of old lot **38** added to lot **46**; the p. **7** diagram depicts 355, 399, and the riverfront/149th Street parcels in their spatial order, including old and new lot lines. Physical p. **8** separately names owners of 355 lots 46/47, 399 lot 100, and four **New York State** parcels. The instrument documents shared or assembled **zoning-lot scope**, not that all seven parcels were the physical 710-unit building site. It confirms why using a zoning-lot union as the building-ground denominator would conflate distinct locations.

By 2023 DOF combined ground into enlarged lot 46, now PLUTO-recorded **120,272 square feet / 710 built units**. That later tax lot includes ground from the separately documented 399 cleanup site; the DEC environmental divide does not rule out shared zoning rights. **Proposed disposition: remain open** on the 710-unit building's economic ground. Do not use either the 20v5 filing-lot-46 **29,200 square feet** alone, the 2021 seven-parcel zoning lot, or the later **120,272-square-foot** lot 46 as an unqualified 2020 building-site area. The decisive next original is the approved job `210182069` site/zoning drawing that locates the building, required open space/parking/access, and any separately retained 399 or 475 phase. The September 2021 declaration does not do that assignment.

## `historical__210180819` and `historical__210180828`: Starhill, 244 + 326 units

The DOB filings are **2020-02-13** for 1600 Macombs Road, **244** units, and **2020-02-18** for 1600 Grand Avenue, **326** units. [Bronx Pro's Phase I page](https://bronxprogroup.com/completed/starhill-phase-1-i-bronx-ny) identifies a **two-phase, two-building, 570-unit** campus with a shared amenities bridge and open space; its approximate **65,000-square-foot** statement refers to the campus. The newly saved [Phase II page](https://bronxprogroup.com/construction/starhill-phase-2-bronx-ny) expressly identifies the **244-unit** phase and calls its site approximately **0.7 acre / 31,000 square feet**, close to later lot **134's 30,879-square-foot** PLUTO area. The official [May 2024 HDC financing memorandum](https://www.nychdc.com/sites/default/files/2024-05/0.%20Approval%20of%20an%20Authorizing%20Resoultion%20relating%20to%20the%20Multi-Family%20Housing%20Revenue%20Bonds-Final%20Signed.pdf), physical p. **18**, identifies one **244-unit, 14-story Phase II building** at 1600 Macombs and its Phase II owner entities. These later project descriptions support the adopted **one 570-unit economic development with two constituent phases**, without moving the 2022/2024 financing dates back to 2020.

The newly archived [executed zoning-lot description, ACRIS `2022031800654005`](https://a836-acris.nyc.gov/DS/DocumentSearch/DocumentImageView?doc_id=2022031800654005), was dated **2022-03-09** and recorded **2022-03-29** as CRFN `2022000132608`. Its physical p. **3** expressly defines one **Combined Zoning Lot** as *then-current* block 2865 lots **134 and 160**. Physical pp. **3–4** give legal courses for those two parcels; the current-lot diagram is on p. **7**. Physical pp. **5–6** then describe the reapportioned **tentative new lot 160** (northern/Grand Avenue side) and **tentative new lot 134** (southern Macombs/Featherbed side). The p. **8** diagram partitions the **same outer perimeter** into those two lots and expressly labels adjacent lots **162 and 129 “NOT INCLUDED IN MERGED ZONING LOT.”** Physical p. **9** lists separate owners of portions of that combined site. Thus the March 2022 original documents the merged project ground and the later phase split, while excluding the retained neighboring parcels; it is more specific than an index entry or a general developer area estimate. It does **not** itself assert that the exact same legal zoning lot had been established at the February 2020 DOB filing date.

The DOF [pre-filing block 2865 map](https://propertyinformationportal.nyc.gov/pdf/home/index/map_library/20286520180216102824), p. **1**, and [2022-04-08 post-reapportionment map](https://propertyinformationportal.nyc.gov/pdf/home/index/map_library/20286520220408135032), p. **1**, match that sequence. **Lot 160 did not yet exist in the 19v2 MapPLUTO vintage**; 20v4–20v5 assigned it zero recorded area after its 2020 split from old lot 162, and 21v1 assigned **4,050 square feet**. The executed declaration's current-lot-160 legal rectangle, **44.50 × 100 feet** on p. **4**, encloses **4,450 square feet**, showing that its interim recorded area was unreliable. The 19v2 old lot 134 recorded **42,600 square feet** despite a saved map polygon of about **65,608 square feet**; 19v2 old lot 162 recorded **12,500 square feet** and includes retained land outside the declaration. Summing the old recorded attributes (**55,100 square feet**) would thus both omit real old-lot-134 ground and wrongly include retained old-lot-162 ground. By 22v1 the two reapportioned project lots record **30,879** (new 134) and **39,184 square feet** (new 160), a **70,063-square-foot later administrative union**; their saved map polygons total about **70,022 square feet**. The differences reflect vintage and recorded-versus-polygon measurement, not a demonstrated extra parcel. The 19v2 retained lots 129 and 162 recorded **55 and 44 residential units**, respectively, but the recorded 2022 diagram excludes both; old lot 134's 88-unit/two-building attribute reflects an earlier condition and does not establish retained dwellings within the new project boundary. The ACRIS index also identifies a **2022-06-16** easement `2022063001534009` on lots 134/160 and a **2024-06-27** Phase II deed `2024070200987001` on lot 134, but those scans remain unseen.

**Proposed disposition:** retain the adopted shared **570-unit economic parent**, with **244 Phase II / 326 Phase I** as separate buildings. The recorded 2022 zoning diagram and DOF map substantiate the **two-lot combined project ground, excluding retained lots 129/162**. **70,063 square feet is defensible as the reconstructed later administrative area of that ground**; it is not claimed as a survey-precision February 2020 area. The 2020 DOB site plans would be needed to establish the earliest filed legal boundary, and the easement/deeds would clarify shared rights. The development evidence does not establish a legal wage-assessment unit.
