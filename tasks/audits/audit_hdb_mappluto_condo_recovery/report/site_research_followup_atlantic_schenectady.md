## 567 Schenectady Avenue / 818 Rutland Road, `post_policy__B01318629-I1`

The saved new-building filing has 402 units. The [October 2025 NYS DEC
application](https://extapps.dec.ny.gov/data/DecDocs/C224448/Application.BCP.C224448.2025-10-29.Complete%20BCP%20Application.pdf),
physical PDF p. 31, describes the then-current proposal as **one 402-unit**
U-shaped residential building, with a greenhouse and courtyard. Its p. 16
describes a roughly 2.13-acre northern site, and p. 26 maps that irregular
site. The [May 2026 NYS HCR board book](https://hcr.ny.gov/system/files/documents/2026/08/may-13-2026-board-book-1_0_0.pdf),
physical pp. 195–196, identifies 818 Rutland, also 567 Schenectady, as one
402-unit building and includes **28 surface parking spaces** in its
construction description. Its p. 201 projects parking income. The HCR
description also says the project acquired “nearly 3 acres”; that acquired
tract includes land reserved for a later phase and is not itself the 402-unit
building ground.

The [June 30, 2026 recorded agreement](https://a836-acris.nyc.gov/DS/DocumentSearch/DocumentDetail?doc_id=2026070900638021),
PDF pp. 3–4, defines the developer's land as block 4602 lots 2 and 3. It
places **Phase I residential and community buildings on Parcel A**, part of
lot 3, and says developer lot 2 will be an **open parking area**. It reserves
Parcel B, another part of lot 3, for Phase II; its legal descriptions appear
at pp. 30–33. Exhibit H p. 37 states these distinct areas:

| Component | Recorded area (sq ft) | Phase I role |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Parcel A / Development Land | 92,709 | 402-unit building, greenhouse, courtyard |
| Developer lot 2 | 12,673 | Open parking area; HCR confirms surface parking in the same 402-unit project |
| Parcel B / Reserved Land | 23,363 | Later Phase II, excluded |
| Owner lots 1 and 5 | 195,658 | Retained institutional land, excluded |

**The documented Phase I economic-parent ground is 105,382 sq ft** when its
dedicated ancillary parking lot is included: 92,709 + 12,673. The building
and courtyard component alone is 92,709 sq ft. The supervisor accepted the 105,382-sq-ft
boundary in the audit source table; the combined 324,403-sq-ft zoning lot is
not Phase I ground.
The [companion zoning declaration](https://a836-acris.nyc.gov/DS/DocumentSearch/DocumentDetail?doc_id=2026070900638022),
PDF pp. 3 and 5, and [declaration
2026070900638020](https://a836-acris.nyc.gov/DS/DocumentSearch/DocumentDetail?doc_id=2026070900638020),
pp. 3–4, confirm the wider four-lot zoning/ownership context, not a different
Phase I allocation. The agreement does not print DOB job `B01318629-I1`; the
site, developer, and exact 402-unit description crosswalk it to the DEC and
HCR originals. These 2026 recorded instruments are later than the new-building
filing and must remain dated as such. A 2022 DEC Phase I report described an
earlier three-building concept on about 101,951 sq ft and does not define this
402-unit filing. The current DOF lot-3 `LAND_AREA` attribute of 26,249 sq ft
also conflicts with the recorded development parcels and should not replace
the legal allocation.

## 698 Atlantic Avenue, `historical__321590532`

The [2017 DOF tax map](https://propertyinformationportal.nyc.gov/pdf/home/index/map_library/30112020170222135621)
shows the broad old rail-yard lot 1 at the May 2020 filing, while the
[August 2022 successor map](https://propertyinformationportal.nyc.gov/pdf/home/index/map_library/30112020220816100301)
separates a western **260 × 200-foot, 52,000-sq-ft** lot 1 from lots 19 and
35 farther east. [Empire State Development's site
map](https://esd.ny.gov/sites/default/files/media/document/12324-AYCDC-Project-Update.pdf),
PDF p. 4, labels the western site B5 and distinguishes B6/B7. Current exact
address geocoding returns successor lot 1. These originals make 52,000 sq ft
a physically bounded **B5 candidate** rather than the production
122,384-sq-ft old-lot envelope, but do not establish that the entire
successor lot was the ground of historical DOB job 321590532.

Official DOB B-SCAN search indexing points to job 321590532's AI-1 scan
`ES545689801` and architectural sheet `ES288044521`, with B5/rail-yard
references. The official B-SCAN servlet returned an unsuccessful-document
message in the browser and 403 on direct retrieval; the actual scans were
not inspected or archived. The necessary missing fact is a **viewable
job-specific 2020 plan or recorded B5 legal allocation** tying that job to
the 52,000-sq-ft rectangle and ruling out a different filing-era split. The
2018 ACRIS cooperation agreement in the earlier source packet covers B5–B7
together, so its whole premises are not a B5 measurement. The 682-unit HDB
count remains intact.
