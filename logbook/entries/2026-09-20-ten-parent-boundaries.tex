\section{20 September 2026: Ten more historical parent boundaries}

The next ten historical parents represent six development sites. Original
recorded instruments, city tax maps, and environmental records support physical
ground allocations for seven current parents. Three allocations remain open.
These are observed development grounds; later records do not establish initial
ownership or a legal wage-assessment unit. Administrative housing counts and
filing dates remain the source measurements.

The substantive link finding is Bedford Square. Four filings at 2201 Beverly,
2366 Bedford, 2363 Bedford, and 158 Lott arrived within 31 days and contain
296, 354, 132, and 95 housing units. The DEC remedial investigation describes one
four-building development on the former Sears parcels. The developer identifies
those four addresses as its buildings A--D, and the DEC agreement documents the
later building-specific owners within the common project. The audit accepts
one \textbf{877-unit economic parent}. Applying this decision will reduce the
parent count by three while preserving the four constituent filings and all
877 units.

The recorded west-block deed descriptions allocate 43,413.10 and 73,457.69
square feet to the two new buildings and separately retain the 53,905.83-square-foot
Sears parcel. The east-block DOF subdivision names both DOB jobs and partitions
the complete earlier lot into their two sites. Counting the whole earlier east
lot at 60,650 square feet once gives combined development ground of
\textbf{177,520.79 square feet}. The later east administrative areas sum to
60,726; that 76-square-foot vintage difference is retained as a measurement
comparison. It does not leave the site's coverage unresolved.

At 16 Dupont, the recorded agreement supplies 9,011 square feet of conveyed
lot-6 ground and 11,890 square feet on adjoining lot 10. A January 2022 NYC
Office of Environmental Remediation fact sheet explicitly identifies both lots,
the applicant, and the proposed residential tower, with an outlined site map.
The adopted legal sum is \textbf{20,901 square feet}, compared with 12,194 in the
current production characteristics. The agreement separately retains a street
strip. The OER notice rounds the site to 20,900; later DOF records give 20,890.

For the already accepted 570-unit Starhill development, the March 2022 original
zoning declaration describes and partitions the combined ground into the two
phase lots. It explicitly excludes neighboring lots 129 and 162. Later
administrative areas of 30,879 and 39,184 square feet give \textbf{70,063 square
feet} of reconstructed development ground. The older area attributes are
inconsistent, so this is labeled as a later administrative measurement of the
supported boundary, rather than a February 2020 surveyed area.

Eagle's three filings match the 745-unit Site D, but its shared waterfront and
access land remains unallocated. Exterior's newly inspected declaration defines
a seven-parcel zoning lot spanning separate properties and state land; it does
not locate the 710-unit proposal's ground. At Story, the January 2019 deed and
19v2 records support an approximately 276,733-square-foot ownership parcel,
contradicting the earlier 553,463 attribute, but the building-specific allocation
still needs its approved site plan.

The audit now contains 39 boundary decisions and eleven pairwise membership
decisions, representing seven accepted development groups and two unresolved
pairs. The full current sample has \textbf{135 unresolved parents out of 873},
down from 136. Dupont clears; the six current Bedford Square and Starhill parents
retain pending merger flags until their membership and land are updated
together. This separates completed boundary research from implementation work.
The parent and constituent panels, canonical membership, and calibration
weights have unchanged SHA-256 values relative to the start of this batch.
