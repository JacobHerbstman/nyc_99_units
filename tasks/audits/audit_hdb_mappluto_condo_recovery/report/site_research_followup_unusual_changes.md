# Five unusual DOF-change flags: dated records and physical scope

This is a bounded read of five frozen parent cases. It uses the saved
2026-09-15 primary DOF alteration-book/action and digital map-index snapshot
in `tasks/fetch_dof_tax_map_history` and the audit's derived event,
scope, geometry-overlap, and MapPLUTO tables. The [DOF acquisition
README](../../../fetch_dof_tax_map_history/README.md) records the live service,
original ZIPs, pagination, and checksums. No new source was downloaded for
these five because the saved DOF transaction authority and mapped parcel
intersections answer the immediate classification question. Recorded and
mapped square-foot figures below are different measures.

The `unusual_change` flag means the action is neither a simple one-to-one
lot split nor an ordinary merger in the automated classification. It does
not itself establish a larger economic parent or a building-footprint
change. Cohort dates and later administrative actions remain separate.

The first two parents need a filing-date site plan before treating their
later merged land as an economic parent. Eagle Street needs a cohort-date
map and filing schedule because the 2018 same-day apportionment preceded
the job. Crown Street's external two-lot union is **strongly supported** by
near-total measured overlap, but its May 4 lot-9 drop/recreation and
internal building allocation require separate treatment. Direct retrieval
of the final 2022-05-04 map PDF failed during this follow-up, so the final
map is cited from the saved official `dtm_8` index rather than a visual
inspection of its PDF. East 181st Street's **site membership is resolved** as
the same two lots, while the later block survey may have moved a small
edge. Its sibling-filing question is separate and remains open.
