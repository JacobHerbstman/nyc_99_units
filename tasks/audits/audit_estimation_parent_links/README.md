# Review estimation parents, companions and refilings

The current complete-characteristic, A/B, 50-plus-unit casebook contains 67 multi-constituent parents (23 historical and 44 post-policy), with 155 source constituent rows. Original reviews of 76 parents are preserved in `code/parent_evidence.csv` and mapped to current membership through filing IDs. The additional Nostrand Land case has a saved-record review and an explicit pending independent-corroboration note.

`review_parents.R` assembles each case from saved membership, accepted link evidence, HDB records and DOB filings. It distinguishes common development, distinct buildings, complete boundaries and unit measurement. Public-source judgments retain their URLs and limitations. Adopted production decisions belong to `parent_opportunities_manual`; the original audit evidence is not an override.

`screen_neighbors.R` screens all eligible parents, including singletons, against positive-unit new-building records within 200 metres and 365 days of a member, plus same-lot matches regardless of distance. Members already inside a parent are excluded. The current queue has 1,210 parent–filing pairs. The original 137 neighbor reviews are retained and remapped; current manual rejections are applied with their qualifications. Other candidates remain explicitly unadjudicated. Missing coordinates, recorded source coverage and the date window limit this screen. Different owner labels do not prove unrelated beneficial ownership.

`review_refilings.R` displays the production rule's 12 automatically detected replacement pairs in 11 parents. Their withdrawn records no longer add 464 duplicate units. Six pairs account for the 368 units originally flagged in five estimation parents. All original parent IDs, cohort dates, raw filing dates and per-record unit fields remain unchanged. Four former multi-constituent parents now contain one additive building. `output/refilings.csv` gives every pair's dates and unit counts; its standard report checks the saved keys.

`trace_companions.R` checks the seven accepted companions against staged linkage fields and geometry coverage and verifies final shared-parent membership. The Third Avenue PNG is an unaltered rendering of October 2025 RAWP page 77. The detailed source review in `report/ten_case_deep_review.md` preserves the investigation and identifies which earlier recommendations have been implemented. Common membership does not establish legal wage treatment or a first-filed unit schedule.

Inputs retain HDB 25Q4 and DOB July 2026 vintages. Public PDFs are acquired by `download_parent_review_documents`; no live searches occur in analysis builds. Outputs have deterministic reports. Documented-unit fields expanded the constituent report from 61 to 65 columns; the original-date and refiling fields now bring it to 68.
