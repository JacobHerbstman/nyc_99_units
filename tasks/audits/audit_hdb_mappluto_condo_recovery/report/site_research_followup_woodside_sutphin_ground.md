## Woodside: common ground resolved

`historical__420665845` is the 295-unit West Building at 46-09 69th Street, filed June 1, 2019; `historical__420665952` is the 183-unit East Building at 46-10 70th Street, filed June 14, 2019. The existing audit decision accepts their 478-unit economic-parent link, pending coordinated implementation. The [executed NYSDEC agreement](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_followup_2026-09-20_six_historical/woodside_bcp_agreement_2019.pdf), physical PDF pages 1 and 3, names both addresses and QB Development Owner LLC, and lists exactly six old Queens block 2432 lots—8, 9, 21, 41, 44, 50—to become two successor lots 8 and 9. The agreement's Exhibit A (PDF page 7) and newly archived [DOF maps](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_followup_2026-09-20_woodside_sutphin/README.md) show the six-lot boundary and exclude neighboring lots 1, 23, and 39. The November 2021 [NYSDEC Site Management Plan](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_followup_2026-09-20_six_historical/woodside_smp_2021.pdf), physical PDF page 14, states that the resulting two-parcel site is **71,862 square feet** and the redevelopment comprises one 15-story mixed-use building and one 12-story residential building. This is direct two-building development evidence, not just a cleanup perimeter.

The archival **18v2beta** MapPLUTO lots already selected by `output/dof_parent_parcels.parquet` add exactly to the DEC ground:

The frozen DOF events show transaction 89475 merging those six on March 17, 2020, then transaction 89489 apportioning lot 8 into retained 8 and new 9; its authority cites April 12, 2019 deed CRFNs **2019000117973** (lots 8, 9, 21, 41, 44) and **2019000117972** (lot 50), plus a March 17, 2020 James C. Weed survey. The old [DOF map](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_followup_2026-09-20_woodside_sutphin/woodside_dof_tax_map_2018-10-01.pdf) was effective through the 2020 action; its six polygons match the BCA list. The [successor map](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_followup_2026-09-20_woodside_sutphin/woodside_dof_tax_map_2020-03-17.pdf) shows the two resulting ground lots. The original deeds and Weed survey are cited in DOF's map action, but were not independently retrieved here; they are no longer needed to reconcile the *total* six-lot historical area because the source parcels, DEC total, and map boundary coincide exactly. They would be needed to assign exclusive ground to each building. Condo billing lot 7502 is not a third ground parcel.

**Adjudication:** a combined 478-unit economic parent has one fully corroborated **71,862-sf historical ground union**, counted once. This does not prove the two DOB filings are one legal wage-assessment unit, nor divide the common land between them. The two filing dates and administrative units remain 295 and 183.

## Sutphin / 95th: complete physical partition; economic link remains a decision

`historical__Q00657653` is the 524-unit 94-15 Sutphin Boulevard building on retained Queens block 9999 lot 1; `historical__Q00657656` is the 521-unit 147-35 95th Avenue building on new lot 40. Both source jobs were filed February 8, 2022 and share an architect and DOB owner-business entry. DOF transaction **94895**, effective January 31, 2022, apportioned the old lot 1 into lots 1 and 40 before those filings. Its recorded authority cites a January 10, 2022 Richard Tom survey and January 13, 2022 CRFN **2022000020183**. The already saved [official tax map](../../download_parent_review_documents/output/dof_map_40999920220131162425.pdf), PDF page 1, visually shows the split and excludes adjacent block 9999 lot 9. The earlier 21v1 MapPLUTO reference has old lot 1 at **60,138 sf**.

The applicant's April 2023 [NY Department of State Site A BOA application](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_followup_2026-09-20_woodside_sutphin/sutphin_site_a_boa_application_2023-04-06.pdf), physical PDF page 4, identifies lot 40 as 30,067 sf and says its new 24-story building and footprint cover the entire lot. The applicant's June 2024 [Site B BOA application](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_followup_2026-09-20_woodside_sutphin/sutphin_site_b_boa_application_2024-06-07.pdf), physical PDF pages 4–5, similarly assigns one 24-story building to all of retained lot 1 and calls the Site A building a **neighboring project developed by many of the same parties**. Its site plan is at physical PDF page 30. The already saved [Site B NYSDEC BCP application](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_large_parents_2026-09-16/94-15_Sutphin_Site_B_BCP_Application.pdf), physical PDF page 60, contains an August 2, 2023 access agreement: Site A owner Sutphin Boulevard Equities LLC contracted to sell Site B to 95th Avenue Equities LLC. That documents related parties and separate site control, not an integrated building footprint.

The BOA Site B form uses an **approximate 30,047 sf**, 24 below the **30,071 sf** recorded for retained lot 1 in NYC DOF final assessments for FY2023–27 (`output/dof_subset_assessment_history.csv`, original DOF response archived in `download_parent_review_documents/code/dof_subset_validation_2026-09-15/assessments.json`). Lot 40 is **30,067 sf** in those same DOF records, matching its BOA form. The two authoritative successor assessment areas sum **30,071 + 30,067 = 60,138 sf**, exactly the archived pre-split lot area. This resolves the complete physical allocation: one full successor lot per building, and no unexplained residual ground. The 30,047 figure should not be used as an exact land replacement.

**Adjudication:** land can be assigned individually at **30,071 sf (Sutphin)** and **30,067 sf (95th)** without a parent merger. If a common economic parent is separately accepted, its combined physical ground is **60,138 sf once**, with administrative units 524 + 521 = 1,045. The common filing date/team, original parcel, and related parties support coordination, but the first-party BOA applications explicitly call the buildings separate neighboring projects and allocate full separate lots. I found no first-party joint development agreement, common amenity/garage plan, cross-collateral deed, or explicit two-building project statement that would settle economic membership. The environmental Site A/Site B labels and later financing phases alone cannot decide it. Neither partition implies one legal wage-assessment unit.
