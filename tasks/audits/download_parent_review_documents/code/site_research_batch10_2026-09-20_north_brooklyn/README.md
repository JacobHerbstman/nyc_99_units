# North Brooklyn original-source acquisition, 20 September 2026

This folder preserves original public agency records for the 745-unit Eagle/West parent and the 381-unit 16 Dupont filing. `sources.csv` gives a source URL, acquisition date, byte count, and SHA-256 for every source file. The `acris_legal_*` and `acris_master_*` files are NYC ACRIS Open Data index responses, not document images. The two ACRIS PDFs were exported with **Save All** from the public Document Image Viewer; the URL in the manifest is the document-detail entry point. DOF map PDFs and assessment HTML are original public DOF responses. Do not replace an original with OCR or a rendered page.

The ACRIS master query for `crfn=2018000185733` maps the Eagle Subparcel D agreement to image ID `2018053100208011`. That original had not been exported when this note was written. A later 118-page Dupont development-rights document, image ID `2021112201527002`, was also attempted, but the public viewer returned HTTP 429 during Save All. Neither unviewed document is treated as evidence of a ground allocation. The later Dupont building boundary is instead stated explicitly in the original OER fact sheet below.

Decisive reviewed pages:

- `waterboard_2021-05-10.pdf`: p. 31 labels Site D **745 units** on the agency's Greenpoint Landing plan, separate from other phases; pp. 23, 26–27 document former 2494/6's 11,714-square-foot area, 2,703-square-foot street portion, and 9,011-square-foot conveyance. The affordable H1/H2 phase is offsite.
- `eagle_certificate_2019050900102007.pdf`: pp. 3–4 owner and Subparcel D CRFN; pp. 5–6 distinguish tax lots 2, 3, 10, 21 and legally describe lot 21; p. 8 draws their arrangement. This is a broader zoning-lot certificate, not a job-ground schedule.
- `dof_map_30247220190304110524.pdf`: p. 1 shows the Eagle map effective March 4, 2019, before the three March DOB filings. The 2018 and 2022 map PDFs bracket the reapportionment and later splits.
- `dof_assessment_3024720021_2020.html` and `_2021.html`: 2019–20 and 2020–21 administrative lot-21 area **160,511 square feet**, conflicting with the cohort map polygon of about 103,391 in the frozen geometry audit; not adopted as physical ground.
- `planning_eagle_demapping_2025_250033.pdf`: pp. 1–2 and 4 plus the Brooklyn Borough President appendix explain that BOP Greenpoint D owns the mapped Eagle Street paper street west of West Street; it functions as public waterfront access and vehicular access for adjacent developments. This later statement does not assign the whole access strip to the 745-unit parent.
- `dupont_development_rights_2021070601644010.pdf`: p. 39 Exhibit C gives Lot 6 Land **9,011 square feet**, pp. 45–46 lot 10 **11,890**, and p. 52 city-retained streetbed **2,703**; pp. 8–9 distinguish the conveyance from retained lot 10.
- `planning_greenpoint_2013_140019.pdf`: pp. 11–12 contemplate a then-future 40-story tower partly on lot 6 and partly on Southern GLA land; the 2013 concept does not fix the October 2021 381-unit job's site.
- `nyc_cb1_2022-02-08_dupont_cleanup_notice.pdf`: **PDF p. 159** is NYC OER's January 2022 fact sheet. It identifies 16 Dupont, applicant Greenpoint Landing Lot 6 LLC, block 2494 **lots 6 and 10**, the 20,900-square-foot site, and the planned 40-story mixed-use building. This explicitly links both former lots to the observed building, unlike the broader 2021 rights agreement. The 24.6 MB original was saved from NYC's public CB1 meeting packet; direct retrieval required a browser user agent.
- `dof_map_30249420211110150453.pdf` and `dof_map_30249420220214140934.pdf`: p. 1 each, separate old 6/10 and successor merged 6 respectively.
- `dob_bis_eagle_jobs.json`: doc 01 for each of 321387021, 321387076, and 321387147; all are BOP Greenpoint D, 2472/21 and sum to 745 units.
- `dob_now_dupont_job.json`: `B00604543-I1`, 16 Dupont, 2494/6, Greenpoint Landing Lot 6 LLC, 381 units, filed October 26, 2021.
- `cityrecord_greenpoint_2021.html`: public hearing notice states the lot-6 disposition and development-rights transfer support offsite affordable H1/H2. It is not an allocation of the later 381-unit building.

The adjacent waterfront/park parcel role for Eagle Site D remains open. Dupont's two-lot gross building boundary is directly established by OER. The root audit accepted the recorded legal parcel sum of **20,901 square feet** (9,011 + 11,890); OER states a rounded 20,900, and the later DOF administrative area is 20,890.
