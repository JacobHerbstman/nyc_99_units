# Audit HPD 485-x registration linkage

This audit links HPD's public building registrations to initial DOB New
Building jobs and summarizes registration patterns for the 2025 exact-99
buildings under two parent definitions:

- the conservative symmetric parent used by the preferred no-notch model; and
- the broader candidate economic parent from the developer-opportunity audit.

The exact-99 building ledger and parent summary also flag observations in the
preferred 180-day model sample. This keeps a 99-unit component of a larger
parent separate from a parent whose total is exactly 99 units.

The descriptive analysis treats an Option B registration reporting fewer than
100 units as evidence of intended separate sub-100 treatment. This is a
maintained working assumption, not evidence that HPD approved a final
application or legally determined a separate Eligible Site.

Repeated HPD submissions are preserved in the response-level linkage. For
building- and parent-level summaries, the audit uses the latest response for a
matched DOB root job, reported DOB NOW root job, legacy BIS job number, or BIN,
in that order.

## Current snapshot

The August 20, 2026 fetch contains 312 responses representing 297 building
keys. The linkage resolves 255 of those keys to the staged DOB initial-filing
panel: 238 through a reported DOB NOW root job and 17 through a unique BIN.
There are no conflicting root-job and BIN matches.

Nine of the 52 individual 2025 DOB filings for exactly 99 units appear in the
public registrations. All nine report 99 units under Option B, and the HPD and
DOB unit counts agree. Three are exact-99 parents in the preferred 180-day
model. The other six are 99-unit components of larger symmetric parents.

The public records corroborate two fully registered multi-building symmetric
parents: three linked components totaling 268 DOB units report 99, 99, and 81
units to HPD, while two linked 99-unit components report 198 units in total.
The broader candidate definition also identifies a four-building Bergen Street
cluster with three 99-unit registrations and one 81-unit registration. These
are strong descriptive evidence of intended separate sub-100 treatment under
the maintained assumption, not final HPD Eligible Site determinations.
