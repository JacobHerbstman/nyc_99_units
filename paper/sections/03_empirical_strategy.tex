\section{Empirical Strategy}

Observed unit count is a policy response, so treatment should be based on pre-policy exposure rather than realized post-policy units. The initial design will simulate feasible unit counts for each parcel using pre-2024 donor projects on similar lots. Parcels with high simulated probability of falling just above 100 units are more exposed to the 485-x notch. The main outcomes are land prices and developer avoidance responses.
