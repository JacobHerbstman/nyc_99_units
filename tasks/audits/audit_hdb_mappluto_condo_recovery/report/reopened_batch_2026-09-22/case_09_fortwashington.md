# Reopened case 09: 524 Fort Washington Avenue / 29 Overlook Terrace

**Recommendation for root adjudication:** Classify the 2005 filing as an **unbuilt predecessor**, not a separate completed 108-unit building. DOB BIS `104126447` was an earlier, partly excavated proposal for the same tower site later proposed under DOB NOW `M00775662` (164 units). Keep `historical__M00775662`, its 2022-10-20 date, 164 **23Q4 Housing Database** units, and its single constituent filing. Do not add 108 units, and do not treat a permit as a completion certificate. The 2012 city record says no tower footings or foundations had been completed. **Recover the whole earlier four-lot archival site: 45,971 sq ft land and 24,839 sq ft of pre-filing nonresidential building floor.** The current 8,890-sq-ft land value covers only the old synagogue tax parcel. DOB's later, exact-job **42,417-sq-ft zoning-site area** is a comparison measure for apparently the same perimeter, not a demonstrated correction to the archival tax area; its **26,132-sq-ft proposed footprint** is another distinct measure. **Confidence: high** on the unbuilt predecessor, same mapped outer site boundary, and archival whole-parcel quantities; **moderate** on applying the 2023 job-specific boundary to the October 2022 reference.

## Why the old clearance became a flag

The [reopened packet](/private/tmp/nyc_reopened_batch_2026-09-22/case_09.json) says the previous review marked this parent supported, then the new filing collision screen found job `104126447`, filed **2005-05-26**, **108** 23Q4 units, at **29 Overlook Terrace**, permitted **2006-04-20**. It directly matches old block 2180 lot 27 and sits within the four-lot earlier envelope. The current filing `M00775662`, **164** units, is on the postmerger lot 27 and described as a 23-story new building. This is a legitimate shared-land flag, not evidence of 108 previously completed apartments. The 23Q4 inclusive/inactive source supplies historical proposal counts; it is not a certificate of occupancy or a basis to replace the archived 164-unit count with an outside number.

The [2012 Manhattan Community Board 12 resolution](https://www.nyc.gov/html/mancb12/downloads/pdf/land_use__reso_opposing_421a_application_for_29_overlook_terrace.pdf), PDF pp. **1–2**, explicitly calls the older undertaking the **29 Overlook Terrace / 524 Fort Washington Avenue** project and describes a 23-story proposal. It says work begun in 2007 had not produced the new building's footings or foundations by February 2012; only rock removal and four one-story subway-entrance columns were visible (p. 2). Its p. 1 reference to “Block 2189” conflicts with the city's block 2180 parcel records and appears to be a transcription error; the address and lot relationship, rather than that block number, is used here. A [2011 New York Supreme Court opinion](https://www.nycourts.gov/reporter/pdfs/2011/2011_33461.pdf), PDF pp. **5–6** (printed pp. 3–4), places the financed high-rise on the addressed Overlook, West 184th, and Fort Washington properties and describes the 2007 start followed by a 2009 loan default. The court record confirms the shared project history, while the community-board inspection supplies the direct physical noncompletion evidence. The [selling broker's February 2022 statement](https://arielpa.nyc/news/press-releases/ariel-property-advisors-closes-a-noteworthy-12-million-sale-at-29-overlook-terrace-one-bennett-in-hudson-heights) also says the old tower had not been built and the land had remained vacant since the 2008 financial interruption. Its historical 114-unit marketing count is comparison evidence only.

There **was** a distinct preexisting building: the Fort Tryon Jewish Center synagogue on lot 27. Its [own history](https://www.ftjc.org/our-history.html) says the 2008 renovation was not completed and the building was sold in 2021; it later relocated. The synagogue is neither the 108-unit older tower nor proof that the new tower has been built. The old MapPLUTO row records it as `M1` with zero residential floor. Whether the synagogue shell was demolished after the filing is not established by the pre-filing covariate or the later assessment row.

These are proposal floor quantities, not the amount of preexisting floor consumed or retained. Do not assign `24,839` to the prior tower footprint or treat the postmerger building-area field as proof of its physical retention after construction.
