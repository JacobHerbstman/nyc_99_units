## What this establishes

The five cases support our original economic-parent links. The strongest evidence is a filing explicitly identifying its companion as part of one zoning lot with a common owner and applicant; joint-development financing and reporting add corroboration. They do not validate all ten 99+99 parents, other parent links, or the completeness of the parent boundary. No percentage confidence is inferred from this selected handful.

Before structural estimation, the remaining five exact-198 parents should receive the same case review, followed by larger repeated-99 groups and a sample of ordinary historical and recent links. Checking accepted links detects false combinations; checking nearby unlinked filings is also needed to detect missed companions. Membership, additive-versus-replacement status, observation cutoff, and separate legal treatment must remain separate judgments.
