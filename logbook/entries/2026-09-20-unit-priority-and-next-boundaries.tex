\section*{2026-09-20: Recorded zero dwellings, and the next boundary batch}
\textit{Agent-drafted research entry.}

Metropolitan Park's 1,000 hotel rooms had entered both residential panels and
the reweighting/pilot sample. The error arose in our source-selection code:
it filtered Housing Database records by a six-unit minimum and land-data
availability before choosing a unit count. A recorded zero Class A count
therefore became a missing match, triggering the positive DOB fallback.
The initial DOB description said gaming facility, so the separate hotel text
check missed the project. DCP's 25Q4 source explicitly records zero Class A
units and 1,000 hotel rooms; the state-filed casino application corroborates
that distinction.

The producer now reads Class A units from the unfiltered staged HDB table.
A recorded zero remains zero; DOB supplies units only when HDB's count is
missing. Zero-Class-A source filings remain in membership with their original
DOB counts, links, and filing/refiling dates, but contribute no residential
units or constituents. This applies the existing administrative outcome to
all matched jobs. It does not label community-facility beds as apartments or
substitute an outside documentary unit count.

The 50-plus-unit reweighting sample changes from 316 to 312 post-period
parents, from 43,807 to 42,341 units, and from 25 to 24 parents above 300.
Four entirely zero-Class-A parents drop: Metropolitan Park, Mermaid,
Meserole, and Jamaica. A Bronx parent becomes 69 rather than 69+27, and
another filing changes from 81 to 80. The latter accounts for one of the
1,466 corrected units. A separate ten-unit filing becomes five and falls
below the canonical six-unit minimum; it was never in the 50-plus sample.
The broader panel also drops a 233-unit DOB fallback already excluded from
the rental sample. Historical membership and units are unchanged. The post
reweighting sample retains 39 exact-99 parents and nine exact-99 pairs.
The hotel's effect was on the upper tail, normalization, and calibration
composition, rather than the number of exact-99 observations. Refitting the
existing flat-charge pilot gives $\kappa=0.115$ and organization scale 1.2,
with 546 model-implied units lost. The direct reweighted unit gap is 4,471;
that aggregate difference remains unsuitable for policy attribution. The existing
plots, weighting diagnostics, and pilot outputs are refreshed from the
corrected panels; the land and membership review still limits interpretation.

The next documentary batch establishes five additional individual building
boundaries. Penn South's recorded lease bounds a 247-by-163-foot parcel,
40,261 square feet, within the earlier 393,100-square-foot campus. The St.
James/Jerome deed and housing ground lease describe the same stepped parcel;

Sutphin and 95th each occupy a complete successor parcel: 30,071 and 30,067
square feet. Together these exhaust the earlier 60,138-square-foot site.
Their shared filing date and development parties require a separate economic

Kingsbrook/Schenectady's agreement separates the 92,709-square-foot Phase I
building and courtyard site, the 12,673-square-foot parking tract, the 23,363

The observed opportunity therefore has 105,382 square feet including its
ancillary parking. The nearly three-acre acquisition includes the reserved
future phase and is a different quantity. Woodside's previously accepted
478-unit parent has a verified six-lot archival union of 71,862 square feet,
matching the official two-building site description exactly; it must be

52,000-square-foot candidate still needs an accessible job-specific boundary.
