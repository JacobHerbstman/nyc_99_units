# Summarize Opportunity Site Sales Panel

Audits the opportunity-site lot-quarter sales panel.

Outputs are CSV diagnostics only. They summarize the frozen opportunity universe, capacity-threshold sensitivity, exact-BBL DOF sales linkage, ACRIS private-market site-sale linkage, nominal price distributions, same-block unmatched-sale warnings, and hard checks for panel uniqueness and source-count reconciliation.

This audit does not establish that ACRIS captures every commercial real-estate transaction. It verifies that the recorded deed events already in the project are carried into the lot-quarter panel without duplication or silent loss.
