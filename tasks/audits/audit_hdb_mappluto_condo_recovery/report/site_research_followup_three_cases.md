# Three site-scope comparisons: source follow-up (2026-09-20)

This is an evidence handoff for supervisor adjudication, not a change to the
production parent table. Source inventory: [three-case acquisition note](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_followup_2026-09-20_three_cases/README.md).
The audit `parent_site_scope.csv` and `parent_site_other_filings.csv` supply
filing dates, units, existing areas, and the flagged relationships. A transaction
envelope, zoning lot, brownfield boundary, sale package, or air-rights transfer
does not by itself assign all enclosed ground to a housing building.

## 255 and 265 Lorimer Street, Brooklyn

The audit has 255 Lorimer (`historical__321594155`, filed 2019-06-27) at 66
units and 3,925 square feet, and 265 Lorimer (`historical__B00665500`, filed
2022-01-25) at 270 units and 45,129 square feet. The cross-links are only
transaction-envelope/earlier-parcel intersections in the audit, not same-lot
links. The saved [2019 DOF block map](../../download_parent_review_documents/output/dof_map_30307620191110112044.pdf)
and [2022 DOF block map](../../download_parent_review_documents/output/dof_map_30307620220811171416.pdf)
show the later division into distinct lots 30 (255) and 18 (265). The audit's
recorded-lot comparison reports lot 30 at **11,125 square feet** and lot 18 at
**34,411 square feet**. The old 255 parcel union (lots 22+40, 17,925 square
feet) and old 265 lot 18 (45,129 square feet) cannot be added: they refer to
different map vintages and overlap the land later subdivided. Even the sum of
the later lot sizes, 45,536 square feet, needs a filing-plan check before use
as the common economic site's exact ground.

[ODA, the project architect](https://oda-architecture.com/projects/lorimer-house/),
calls Lorimer House and Copper Lofts two connected residential buildings,
specifies 270 and 66 homes, an elevated bridge, common courtyards, and one
client/development team. [The buildings' leasing page](https://lorimerhousebk.com/availability/)
gives Copper residents access to Lorimer House amenities. This is strong
first-party evidence of **one coordinated 336-home development**, while the
buildings and later tax parcels remain distinguishable. For the research parent
definition, the pair is a high-priority join candidate. A DOB ZD1/site plan or
recorded land declaration is still needed for exact common ground and whether
lot 13 or any other ground enters the legal site. ODA's 318,400-square-foot
project size is building area, not ground. Preserve both administrative filing
dates and counts if the economic parent is combined.

## 25-60 31st Street and 26-37 30th Street, Queens

The audit's target `post_policy__Q01359877-I1` is 83 units, filed 2026-03-05,
on block 598 lot 48; the old and production land area are both **7,500 square
feet**, and the DOF geometry comparison says the whole earlier parcel aligns.
`Q01385871` is a separate 23-unit filing at 26-37 30th Street on lot 19,
filed 2026-06-05. The only audit cross-link is transaction 620546's envelope;
there is no direct lot or filing-point overlap. Thus the 23 units cannot be
assigned to lot 48's 7,500 square feet merely because both parcels appear in
one deed/transaction.

There is more than accidental transaction overlap: [the selling broker's own
listing](https://developmentsiteadvisors.com/properties.html?borough=20&lot_sf=6100-Infinity)
markets “25-56 31st Street & 26-37 30th Street” together as a **16,277-square-foot
sale site**. [Z Architecture's 31st Street page](https://zarchitecture.com/portfolio/25-56-31st-street-queens-ny/)
and [30th Street page](https://zarchitecture.com/portfolio/26-37-30th-street-queens/)
show separate rental/commercial and condo/community-facility designs under one
architect. The 31st Street materials use 25-56 while the current DOB entry uses
25-60; a DOB site plan should confirm these are addresses for the same lot-48
building. Those concept pages give 84 and 30 apartments; the audit's newer DOB
filings give 83 and 23, which remain the administrative counts. The broker's
16,277-square-foot package is not automatically the built site's land: it may
include other lots, and the plans are shown as distinct projects. The official
[Queens CB1 Housing Committee agenda](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/queenscb1/downloads/pdf/committee-meeting-agendas/2026/2026-Housing-Memo-Agenda-01_12.pdf)
lists a 25-60 31st Street UAP application, but does not establish the second
building as part of that application. This is a **plausible 106-unit economic
parent**, requiring the two DOB ZD1/site plans and transaction's lot-level deed
or purchaser/ownership documents before a join or area change. Lot 48 alone is
well supported as 7,500 square feet.

## 2752–2768 Webster Avenue and 2740 Webster Avenue, Bronx

The observed 2025-11-21 DOB entries are `post_policy__X01298990-I1`, **510
residential units** at 2768 Webster (block 3273 lot 100 in the audit), and
`X01298988`, **zero residential units**, described in the DOB entry as a new
college dormitory at 2740 Webster (lot 85). The audit joins them only through
a broad transaction envelope. The 2023 [MTA board staff summary](../../download_parent_review_documents/output/webster_mta_property_interests_2023-02-23.pdf)
proposed a *then-current* Longhouse project across 2740 and 2768, about 550
low-income units. That was a different developer/version from the later filings.
The memo distinguishes a proposed **±2,300-square-foot fee conveyance** from
±95,000 square feet of transferable development rights (plus possible 50,000),
a ±32,341-square-foot light/air easement, and a ±4,500-square-foot nonexclusive
surface-use easement. The latter figures are not ground to add to either filing.
The memo authorizes negotiation; it does not prove consummation.

The later [NYPL-hosted 2752–2768 BCP application](https://www.nypl.org/sites-drupal/default/files/2026-01/2752-2768_Webster_Ave_BCP.pdf)
says former **lots 91 and 100 merged**, with a 1.409-acre site in its property
table (about **61,376 square feet**). Its prose has an internally inconsistent
“1.049” acreage and calls the new lot 100. The [December 2025 RI work plan](https://www.nypl.org/sites-drupal/default/files/2026-01/2752%E2%80%932768_Webster_Ave_Dec25_Revised_RIWP.pdf)
calls the same roughly 1.4-acre site new **lot 110**, former lots 91 and 100.
The March 2025 survey reproduced in the [2740 BCP application](https://extapps.dec.ny.gov/data/DecDocs/C203198/Application.BCP.C203198.2026-02-09.Complete_BCP_Application.pdf)
labels lot 91 as 15,520 square feet and lot 100 as 45,858 square feet, whose
sum is **61,378 square feet** (rounding explains the 1.409-acre figure). This
is strong primary evidence that the 2768 development ground extends beyond the
audit's **42,234-square-foot** old lot-100 assignment. A DOB ZD1 or the actual
DOF merger approval should settle the effective tax-lot number and confirm the
exact 510-unit building site, since the environmental site boundary alone is
not enough for final assignment.

The separate [2740 Phase II ESA](https://www.nypl.org/sites-drupal/default/files/2026-03/6_2740_Webster_Phase2_ESA.pdf)
identifies **lots 85, 92, and 93** as a roughly 0.55-acre parcel. It describes
an earlier mixed-use residential/commercial proposal, before the observed
college-dorm filing. The survey embedded in the 2026 BCP application labels
those lots separately from 91 and 100. The same 2740 BCP application names
South Bronx Development I LLC as owner since April 16, 2025 and South Bronx
Development II LLC as prospective developer; the 2768 RI work plan also names
South Bronx Development I LLC as its requestor. This supports coordinated
ownership/investigation but does not turn the dorm parcel into ground for the
510-unit residential building. The [April 2025 Phase I ESA](https://www.nypl.org/sites-drupal/default/files/2026-01/2752%E2%80%932768_Webster_Ave_April_2025_PhaseI_SESI_Part1.pdf)
reprints an older combined marketing summary (2740 ground 25,000 square feet;
2768 ground 57,000; “air rights” 51,978). These are marketing-era estimates,
not the current dorm/residential filing assignment, and the 51,978 is floor-area
rights rather than fee ground. Do not add 2740's ground to the 510-unit
residential building without current site plans showing a common building
project. The dorm's residential count remains zero for this audit.
