# Reopened case 06: 484 East 178 Street

**Status: four-lot land boundary supported, with a filing-design timing
limitation.** The 23Q4 Housing Database record for job `220212918`
(November 2, 2022) has 287 units and filing/HDB BBL `2030430016`. Lot 16 is
real in the filing-date parcel map, but it is only one piece of the development
land. The complete, contiguous site is former Bronx Block 3043 Lots **10, 16,
22, and 23**, later merged into Lot **10**. Thus the filing BBL is an
incomplete *site key*, not evidence for a 7,489-square-foot development.

The [2014 DOF map](https://propertyinformationportal.nyc.gov/pdf/home/index/map_library/20304320140109152807),
effective through August 22, 2024, shows the four adjoining lots occupying the
parcel between Washington Avenue, East 178th Street, and Bathgate Avenue.
It is the applicable DOF map at the filing date and at the 2022-06-01 22v1
reference date. The [2025 DOF map](https://propertyinformationportal.nyc.gov/pdf/home/index/map_library/20304320250507162556)
shows one successor Lot 10 over the same footprint. DOF alteration-book
transaction **304542**, dated **2025-05-07**, names a “Lot Merger” of Lots
10, 16, 22, and 23 and cites deed CRFN `2025000117343` recorded 2025-05-01.
DOF lot-action rows mark 16, 22, and 23 **Dropped** and 10 **Affected**.
ACRIS master document `2025042401044001` links that CRFN to a `CORRD`
record dated April 15 and recorded May 1, 2025; its four legal rows name
exactly Block 3043 Lots 10, 16, 22, and 23. The document code alone does
not establish a conveyance, but it corroborates the four-lot legal set.
This is an ordinary later tax-lot merger, not a condo mapping issue or
evidence that the 2022 site had only Lot 16.

The [NYSDEC C203182 brownfield application](https://extapps.dec.ny.gov/data/DecDocs/C203182/Application.BCP.C203182.2024-12-04.Complete%20BCP%20Application.pdf)
identifies 484 East 178th Street as **40,102 square feet**, current Lot 10,
“formerly Lots 10, 16, 22, and 23” (Attachment A, page 1 of 5; PDF page 17
by page order). This agrees in membership with the DOF maps and is the most
direct documented whole-site area. Its October 2024 attachment describes the
merger before DOF's May 2025 effective date; treat the 2025 DOF transaction as
the operative tax-map change. The [DEC February 2026 fact sheet](https://extapps.dec.ny.gov/data/der/factsheet/c203182cupropeng.pdf)
also describes the site as about 0.920 acres, present Lot 10. A DOB-authored
[ZD1 zoning diagram for job 220212918](https://www.pincusco.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/ZD1-ES443460578-2026_03_31-10_49_35.pdf)
lists 484 E 178th St, Block 3043, Lot 10, BIN 2130952 (sheet 1). It is
dated March 10, 2026 and hosted by a third party, so it corroborates the
job-to-successor-lot link but does not establish the original 2022 design
by itself. The saved 2025 East 178th agreement and 2026 zoning PDF
in `site_research_supervisor_2026-09-16` concern **Block 3068, 619–623 East
178th Street**, a different development; they are not evidence for this case.

There is a documented design revision. The 23Q4 source describes the
November 2022, 287-unit filing as a proposed **ten-story** mixed-use building.
The DEC application's Attachment C, dated October 2024 (PDF page 33),
describes a contemplated **eight-story**, U-shaped affordable residential
building with a courtyard on the four-lot site. The March 2026 DOB ZD1 for
the *same job number* depicts a main roof over the eighth floor and gives
no unit count. These later documents support the observed development land
boundary under the project's later-document convention. They do **not**
justify changing the 23Q4 unit count, attributing later design floor area
to the 2022 filing, or asserting that the building footprint was unchanged.
No retrieved record positively shows that the land boundary grew from Lot
16 to four lots after the 2022 filing; the original 2022 zoning plot remains
unavailable.

The four 22v1 MapPLUTO lot areas sum to **40,104 square feet**, only two
square feet above DEC's documentary **40,102**. Treat 40,102 as the
documented survey/site area and 40,104 as the reproducible 22v1 parcel sum;
do not silently force them to match. Lot 16 alone captures **18.7%** of the
22v1 four-lot area and omits 32,615 square feet. All four 22v1 rows have
`bldgarea=0`, `resarea=0`, and `builtfar=0`; they are class V0 and have no
`yearbuilt`. DEC also says the previous structures were demolished in 2019
(application Attachment A, page 2; PDF page 18). Thus the earlier **existing**
gross floor area and built FAR proposal is **zero** across the whole site.
The 4.2 field is *allowed residential FAR*, not existing built FAR or a
measured floor area. No floor-space allocation among these four lots is needed
if the whole development site is attributed to this single parent. Do not
substitute proposed construction floor area for earlier existing floor area.

**Proposed audit treatment:** retain filing BBL `2030430016` as received;
crosswalk this parent to successor `2030430010` and reference parcels
`2030430010;2030430016;2030430022;2030430023` at 22v1. For land, report
both the 40,102-square-foot DEC area and the 40,104-square-foot 22v1 sum,
with the latter suitable for an exact parcel-panel reconstruction. For
earlier built floor area, use zero. Keep the 23Q4 287-unit observation fixed;
later project descriptions and other Housing Database releases do not
override it. There is no support here for borrowing land or floor area from
neighboring Lots 8, 30, 57, or another block.

**Remaining temporal limit:** the original 2022 BIS/DOB zoning plot or
contemporaneous site plan for job `220212918` was not recovered. It would
verify the initial building footprint and zoning-lot declaration, which
matter if the analysis is specifically about the proposal as first filed.
The evidence already supports the four-lot land reconstruction for the
observed development; the unavailable original plan is not evidence of an
incompatible smaller site. Identify the original job's proposed floor area
only if a separate planned-intensity measure needs it; it is irrelevant to
22v1 existing gross floor area. This research report proposes the land
crosswalk and leaves production changes to the supervising review.
