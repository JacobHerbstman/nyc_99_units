## Source checks and exact page targets

**GO Broome.** The newly saved [DEIS Chapter 1](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_followup_2026-09-20_broader/go_broome_deis_ch1.pdf) states on physical PDF pages 2–3 that the action facilitates the Suffolk and Norfolk buildings together on Projected Development Site 1, old lots 37+75. It prints the 32,401-sf total and approximate component-lot areas of 7,443 and 24,958. Existing Hong Ning lot 1 and mixed-use lot 95 are separately described. I visually inspected Figure 1-2 on physical page 13, saved as [the plan image](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_followup_2026-09-20_broader/go_broome_deis_site_plan_p13.png): both buildings and their shared courtyard appear next to retained Hong Ning. The plan does not label an exclusive lot boundary for either new building. The [CPC decision](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_followup_2026-09-20_broader/go_broome_cpc_200061a.pdf), PDF pages 2–3 and 7, expressly distinguishes the 51,884-sf zoning lot (lots 1+37+75) from the two-building development on 37+75. PDF pages 35–36 list adopted Dattner plans Z-103.00 and Z-203.00, revised January 17, 2020. Its appendix text says the buildings are linked by a landscaped interior courtyard. The DEIS proposed 488 units; the frozen DOB jobs total 378+117=495. That difference is proposal versus filing evidence and is **not** an override. The archived MapPLUTO areas for 37+75 sum 32,395, six feet below the DEIS approximation. The jobs were filed March 6 and April 10, 2020, 35 days apart. The [original report](site_research_large_parents.md) contains the earlier textual screen.

**Sutphin / 95th.** The saved [Site A application](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_large_parents_2026-09-16/147-35_95th_Site_A_BCP_Application.pdf), physical PDF page 27, calls current lot 40 a 30,067-sf parcel and says its proposed 23-story building encompasses the site; the property narrative and tax exhibit identify it as one part of former lot 1. The saved [Site B application](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_large_parents_2026-09-16/94-15_Sutphin_Site_B_BCP_Application.pdf), physical PDF page 31, says former lot 1 was divided into two in December 2021 and identifies current lot 1 as a 30,047-sf site; the property narrative places it along Sutphin and 94th/95th Avenues. The audit's two jobs share February 8, 2022 and identify each other as a direct other filing. The archived DOB response, described in [the original report](site_research_large_parents.md), matches architect Joseph Frankl, owner business Heartfelt Townhouse Build, and representative Joel Weiss.

**Chatterton / Olmstead.** I visually compared the saved DOF [January 4, 2018
tax map](../../download_parent_review_documents/output/dof_map_20379720180104164133.pdf)
with the [June 3, 2025 tax map](../../download_parent_review_documents/output/dof_map_20379720250603094306.pdf),
one physical page each. The earlier map has old block 3797 lot 33 at the east
end of the block. The later map shows retained lot 33 as a rectangle along
Chatterton Avenue, 200.16 by 105.58 feet, and new lot 70 as the surrounding
L-shaped remainder reaching Olmstead Avenue and Bruckner Boulevard. The
saved subset review records DOF transaction **320942** as explicitly naming
NB **X00702588** for retained lot 33. The [original DOF assessment
response](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/dof_subset_validation_2026-09-15/assessments.json)
reports final FY2027 land areas 21,133 for 2070 Chatterton/lot 33 and 39,996
for 1017 Olmstead/lot 70, both with the same HP Bruckner Housing Development
Fund Co. owner. Lot 33's earlier final FY2026 assessment still reports
60,979: the area field lagged the split. The two successor areas sum 61,129,
150 above the old 60,979 recorded value; these dated assessments should not
be represented as an exact arithmetic partition of the historical attribute.
The audit's DOB jobs are X00702588, 79 units filed May 17, 2022, and
210180105, 265 units filed September 10, 2019. The newly saved [Aufgang
Architects project page](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_followup_2026-09-20_broader/bruckner_heights_aufgang.html)
calls Bruckner Heights a two-building development, names those two addresses,
and gives exactly 79 and 265 units plus parking, outdoor recreation areas,
and green roofs for both. This is first-party evidence for one coordinated
observed project, though it is a 2026 description rather than either original
filed plan. Its square-foot figures are building floor area, **not** land.
The physical split supports excluding Olmstead's lot 70 from an individually
measured Chatterton building parcel and vice versa. The architect description
supports one coordinated *physical* complex, but the DOB filing dates are
September 10, 2019 and May 17, 2022, beyond the adopted 365-day cohort window.
It therefore does **not** support merging the two cohort parent IDs. Evaluate
common ground from a site plan rather than assigning it by the architect's
development label. Separate legal wage treatment is not established.

The evidence above supports **combined physical scope** for GO Broome and

parcel but an unresolved historical land allocation. The Degraw/Sackett and
Noble/Oak ground measures remain open. None of these documents alone assigns
