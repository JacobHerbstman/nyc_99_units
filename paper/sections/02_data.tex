\section{Data}

The core data are linked at the parcel or project level. The first source is the DCP Housing Database, which records DOB-approved housing projects since 2010. PLUTO and MapPLUTO provide parcel characteristics, zoning, geography, and geometry. ACRIS provides property transactions and ownership histories. DOB BIS and DOB NOW filings support applicant, owner, architect, and timing checks. HPD 485-x materials identify program rules and, if available, project-level registration records.
