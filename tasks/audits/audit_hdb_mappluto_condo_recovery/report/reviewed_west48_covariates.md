# West 48th Street: complete land-covariate review, September 21, 2026

The 509 West 48th Street correction is ready to apply. The certified March 2022
HPD environmental assessment, physical page 19, distinguishes the approximately
21,923-square-foot western development site from the full 47,027-square-foot
project parcel and retained eastern open space. Its site plan and subsequent
DOF partition identify the same boundary. The successor administrative area is
21,924 square feet; the one-square-foot difference from the agency approximation
is preserved in the source review.

The actual weighting reference is MapPLUTO **20v8**, BBL **1010770029**. It
records area **57,027**, building floor **0**, residential FAR **6.02**, broad
FAR **6.5**, zoning **R8**, and land use **11** (vacant land). The 57,027 area
conflicts with the agency's whole-parcel measurement and is retained only as
the reference-row check. Apply the 21,924 development area. The whole source
parcel has no recorded building floor, so allocating this smaller ground
requires no building-floor imputation or proportional allocation. Retain its
recorded zoning and use. The assessment describes a below-grade Amtrak rail
right-of-way on the development site; the pipeline's vacant-land category is
the frozen PLUTO classification, not a claim that the land had no prior use.
