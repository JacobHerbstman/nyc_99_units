# Checking the areas of the twelve sampled parents' lots

The ten provisional parcel-area comparisons all match DOF's final assessment
roll for fiscal 2026/27. That establishes their administrative source. Printed
dimensions give a stronger measurement check for six complete parents and
Godwin lot 79. The September 21 merger adds Godwin lot 88 at 5,900 square feet
from the saved assessment; its complete parent totals 18,441. Purdy, Weirfield,
and Lorimer still lack an independent survey calculation.
Sackett's later parcel areas can also be calculated directly. Noble/Oak has
a documented proposed subdivision and a difference between full tax-lot area
and land within the shoreline.

These checks measure parcels at specified dates. Assigning the developer's
original land endowment also requires establishing which of those parcels
belonged to the project when it was proposed. Adopted parent allocations are recorded separately in the production manual table.

PLUTO area, polygon area, and the dimension calculation in separate columns.

## Sackett: different boundaries at different dates

The [2022 NYSDEC property attachment](https://extapps.dec.ny.gov/data/DecDocs/C224222/Application.BCP.C224222.2022-07-06.Major%20Amendment%20Application-Attachment%20A-Property%20Information.pdf)
describes a roughly 38,500-square-foot cleanup boundary; its title survey on
PDF page 11 reports 38,499.76 square feet for then-lot 17. The September 2022
DOF map shows the subsequent 48,500-square-foot parcel. Its area is
`350 × 100 + 135 × 100 = 48,500`.

The September 2025 subdivision creates lots 16, 17, and 47. The recorded
boundary descriptions in the [March 2026 NYSDEC amendment](https://extapps.dec.ny.gov/data/DecDocs/C224222/Agreement.BCP.C224222.2026-03-18.Amendment_No3_Multiple_Changes_563%20Sackett%20Street.pdf),
PDF pages 30-31, imply:

- Lot 17: `265 × 100 − 10 × 10 + 10 × 100 = 27,400` square feet.
- Lot 47: `125 × 100 = 12,500` square feet.
- Both lots together: **39,900 square feet**.

The amendment removes neighboring lot 16. Its DOF map dimensions give
`85 × 100 + 10 × 10 = 8,600` square feet, while its FY2027 assessment incorrectly
reports 12,500. The total calculated from the three lots' map dimensions is
`8,600 + 27,400 + 12,500 = 48,500`, conserving the predecessor's mapped area.
Adding the three assessment entries would instead give 52,400 and create
3,900 square feet on paper.

The manual DOB review adds an important observation: **B00665570-I1 reports a
48,500-square-foot zoning lot and 300 units**. Its district areas are 35,000
in R7X and 13,500 in R6A. This is the live application, modified June 8, 2026;
I1 identifies the initial filing route, not an immutable February 2022 version.

The [August 2025 recorded zoning declaration](https://a836-acris.nyc.gov/DS/DocumentSearch/DocumentDetail?doc_id=2025080501331002)
explicitly includes tentative lots **16, 17, and 47**, formerly lot 17, in
Exhibit III on page 2. Thus the 39,900-square-foot later sale/cleanup site and
the 48,500-square-foot zoning lot are distinct boundaries. The declaration
also differs from the live DOB text's lot list, `17, 47, 41`; that discrepancy
must remain visible. The March 2024 ESD Existing Conditions Report also lists
563 Sackett / lot 17 at 48,500 square feet, using administrative source data.

**48,500 is now supported as the documented zoning-site candidate.** The
production characteristic is 38,500. Moving to 48,500 would add 10,000 square
feet (26.0%), but this audit does not make that replacement. Establishing the
land available at the original February 2022 decision still requires its dated
site/zoning plan. DOB lists a ZD1 document with a January 20, 2022 creation date,
but its contents were not retrievable in this review. The March 2022 NYSDEC
38,500-square-foot description concerns the 560 Degraw cleanup site and is not
that original plan. A February 2022 ACRIS zoning declaration returned by the
old-lot search belongs to the neighboring 545 Sackett entities, so it cannot
be assigned by lot-number matching alone. The August 2026 merger of 47 into 17
also occurs after the July 8 sample endpoint.

## Noble/Oak: proposed subdivision and shoreline

The [city's notice for the June 9, 2026 CB1 meeting](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/brooklyncb1/downloads/pdf/meeting-notices/2026/REVISED-Combined-Public-Hearing-and-Board-Meeting-Notice-06-09-26.pdf),
pages 2-3, explicitly identifies the two-building site and tentative division
of lot 1 into lots 1 and 10. It reports a site area of **173,834 square feet**.
This explains why the proposed lot 10 need not yet appear in DOF's completed
tax map. It is a piece of the existing site, not evidence of an extra full lot.

DOF's full tax-lot polygon measures approximately 297,134 square feet, near its
299,214-square-foot assessment area. MapPLUTO's shoreline-clipped polygon
measures approximately 172,691 square feet, near the city's reported site area.
Less than one square foot of that clipped polygon lies outside the DOF polygon.
The spatial metadata explicitly documents shoreline clipping. The large
geometry difference thus largely reflects inclusion of water area, rather
than competing locations for the development.

The baseline-date check adds another issue: PLUTO 23v3_1's shoreline-clipped
lot-1 polygon measures about **126,049** square feet, versus **172,691** in
25v4. Water exclusion alone therefore does not explain the change across
vintages. DOF transaction 260940, dated March 12, 2025, retains the same BBL
and cites a July 2024 deed and an October 2024 survey.

Manual inspection of the [2024 deed](https://a836-acris.nyc.gov/DS/DocumentSearch/DocumentDetail?doc_id=2024080500318001)
and its [2001 predecessor](https://a836-acris.nyc.gov/DS/DocumentSearch/DocumentDetail?doc_id=FT_3300007672230)
establishes that street land matters. The 2001 Schedule A, page 3, extends to
the centers of Noble, West, and Oak Streets. The 2024 deed identifies the
same premises and conveys the seller's abutting-street rights. Those rights
predate 485-x; the map expansion cannot simply be classified as a new
post-policy land acquisition. However, the 2024 detailed course description
starts at West Street's western edge, and its east–west lengths differ by
30 feet from the 2025 map annotations. The cited survey and filed site plan
must reconcile these street boundaries before the baseline area is finalized.
The 2019 NYSDEC cleanup boundary covers parts of two blocks and excludes a
disputed piece of Oak Street, so its acreage is not a replacement parent area.

The city's 173,834-square-foot site is the documented development-site
candidate. The production input, 252,780, is an earlier full-tax-lot attribute.
These measure different boundaries. Keep ownership, shoreline, zoning site,
and building footprint distinct. This audit does not infer floor-area
entitlements or child-lot areas from the site total. Administrative unit counts
and filing addresses retain their existing source priority.

## What “pre-existing lot size” means here

The intended model input describes the **whole parent site's land before the
organization decision**. If a developer divides that site into two tax lots,
the baseline land area should still count the original land once. It should
not shrink to one child's area, count the full predecessor twice, or silently
include water or unrelated neighboring land.

The current code uses filing-specific earlier PLUTO vintages for historical
parents and fixed PLUTO 23v3_1 for post-policy parents. Later maps and deeds
help identify the earlier footprint; they are not automatically replacement
baseline measurements. For these two cases the manual review supports better
boundary candidates, but does not yet certify both original decision-date
footprints. The [manual source notes](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/dof_subset_validation_2026-09-15/manual_portal_review.md)
record the exact portal paths, document IDs, pages, observations, and access
limits.

## What this establishes for the broader correction

Use DOF transactions to identify predecessor and successor boundaries; attach
an area record that describes those boundaries at the intended date. A reused
BBL or an assessment published after a map change does not guarantee the field
has caught up. Chatterton's final assessment still reports 60,979 in FY2026,
then reports 21,133 in FY2027. Fox changes from 2,500 to 10,015 between FY2023
and FY2024. These are visible administrative lags.

Calculate a discrepancy flag between recorded area and geometry, with a
separate waterfront classification, and examine meaningful discrepancies
against printed dimensions or filed surveys. Do not multiply frontage and
depth for irregular lots, allocate a suspect old area by polygon shares, or
count a full predecessor once for each proposed child. Preserve the competing
numbers until the source or boundary distinction is resolved.

The immediate remaining checks are a survey-based area check for Purdy,
Weirfield, and Lorimer; the original Sackett filing boundary; Noble's street
boundaries in the cited survey; and a consistent onshore-versus-full-tax-lot
definition for weighting. The small review does not
certify all 166 flagged parents. Its ten provisional comparisons and averages
are unchanged, and no production characteristics, weights, or model fits have
been replaced.

Reproduce with `make dof-subset-review`. The linear script

as side effects. `code/dof_lot_measurement_review.csv` records the 16 lot-level
judgments. All new work uses the two existing audit tasks.
