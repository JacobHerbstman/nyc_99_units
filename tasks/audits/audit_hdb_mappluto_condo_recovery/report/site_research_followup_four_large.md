# Four large historical site boundaries: source follow-up (2026-09-20)

This note provides documentary ground evidence for four previously unresolved
historical filings. HDB/DOB filing counts and dates remain the administrative
facts; developer and planning estimates do not replace them. The companion
[source register](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_followup_2026-09-20_four_large/README.md)
records original public URLs, local copies, and SHA-256 hashes. This is
supervisor-facing evidence, not a production data edit.

| Historical parent | Filing ground candidate | Status and chief distinction |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| `historical__121207504`, 180 East 125th Street | 42,540 sq ft | Original job drawing places the building on new lot 27; the neighboring lot 20 is vacant. The 2021 OER plan and 2024 survey agree. |
| `historical__M00641741`, 1440 Amsterdam Avenue | 24,990 sq ft | Existing 12,490-sq-ft private lot 28 received 12,500 sq ft of NYCHA land in two successor lots. Separate 280,000 sq ft is air rights. |
| `historical__M00600672`, 335 Eighth Avenue | 40,261 sq ft | The seven-story successor lot 20 matches the ground-leased project, but the actual lease/survey boundary has not been seen. |
| `historical__M00580473`, 509 West 48th Street | 21,923 sq ft EAS / 21,924 current DOF | Housing disposition lot 29 is distinct from retained park lot 35. Development rights and light/air easement from lot 35 are not housing ground. |

## `historical__121207504`: 180 East 125th Street

The frozen filing records **614 units**, DOB job **121207504**, filed April 8,
2020, on Manhattan block 1773 lot 27. The 20v1 two-earlier-parcel screen
finds lots **20 and 27 totaling 78,769 square feet**, while the later DOF
comparison gives lot 27 **42,540 square feet**. DOF transactions **93296/93297**,
effective **August 5, 2021**, merged earlier lots 20+27 and reapportioned them
back to 20+27, citing a Patrick Benedict James survey dated June 29, 2021.

The [October 2021 NYC OER Remedial Action Work Plan](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_followup_2026-09-20_four_large/east125_oer_rawp_2021.pdf),
archived under NYSDEC's C231160 document repository,
physical PDF pages **6 and 24**, expressly says the 180 East 125th site was
portions of former lots **20 and 27** until that **August 5, 2021**
reapportionment, after which it became **lot 27, approximately 42,540 square
feet**. Its attached S9 Architecture **June 11, 2021 site plan**, physical
page **91**, prints **DOB 121207504** in the title block and shows the proposed
building on eastern **lot 27**; western **lot 20** is labeled *vacant site*.
The red dashed line across the drawing is wider than the building site and
must not itself be treated as the filing's ground. A later NYSDEC [final work
plan](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_followup_2026-09-20_four_large/east125_final_rawp_2025.pdf)
identifies lot 27 as the **42,540-square-foot** site (physical page **14**),
and its appended October 17, 2024 ALTA survey (physical page **96**) measures
**42,540.1 square feet** for lot 27, excluding western 160 East 125th/lot 20
and the firehouse notch. These plans were visually checked.

The historical job-specific drawing and 2021 lot-change narrative support
**42,540 square feet as the 614-unit filing's ground candidate**. The 2025
work plan describes later ownership and a potentially revised building
program, so its parcel survey corroborates the land boundary only. Do not
carry the western vacant/MTA parcel merely because it appears inside a
broader drawing context or earlier two-lot envelope.

**Supervisor adjudication:** Accept **42,540 square feet** as this historical
filing's physical ground, retaining its frozen 614-unit count.

## `historical__M00641741`: 1440 Amsterdam Avenue

The frozen job is **490 units**, filed December 17, 2021, Manhattan block
1984 lot 28. Production has the earlier private lot's **12,490 square feet**;
the 21v1 earlier-envelope screen also sees NYCHA lot 1, producing the
misleading **547,265-square-foot** union. The archived 21v1 parcel record
gives private lot **28 = 12,490 square feet** and NYCHA lot **1 = 534,775**.

NYCHA's [October 17, 2022 first-party closing announcement](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_followup_2026-09-20_four_large/amsterdam_nycha_2022_transfer.html)
states that NYCHA transferred **12,500 square feet of land** *and separately*
**280,000 square feet of development rights** from Manhattanville Houses to
the adjacent private property at **1440 Amsterdam Avenue**. Saved DOF
transaction **95296**, effective March 4, 2022, split NYCHA lot 1 into lots
**1, 10 and 30**, citing the December 27, 2021 Daniela Nicoletti-Rocca survey.
The 22v1 parcel archive records new lots **10 = 10,000** and **30 = 2,500**
square feet, the exact **12,500** transferred ground. DOF transaction
**106502**, effective January 2, 2024, then merged **10+28+30 into lot 28**,
citing 2023 recorded deeds CRFNs **2023000228922/2023000234158**. The saved
[2026–27 DOF assessment](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_followup_2026-09-20_four_large/amsterdam_dof_2027.html)
lists **one 28-story building** at 1440 Amsterdam on lot 28 with **24,990
square feet** of land: 12,490 + 10,000 + 2,500 = **24,990**.

The two transfers and successor merger support **24,990 square feet as the
project's ground candidate**, subject to checking the recorded deeds/survey
if a legal-boundary certification is needed. The other **280,000 square feet
are development rights, not ground**. The retained Manhattanville campus
lot 1 does not belong to this filing. NYCHA's available resident plan also
calls 1440 Amsterdam a *private site* beside Manhattanville; that PDF is
URL-only in the source register because its public host returned HTTP 403 to
direct retrieval.

**Supervisor adjudication:** Accept **24,990 square feet** as physical ground;
keep 280,000 square feet of air rights and retained NYCHA campus land separate.

## `historical__M00600672`: 335 Eighth Avenue

The frozen **188-unit** filing was made October 27, 2021 on Manhattan block
751 lot 20. The archival 21v1 parcel screen instead matches parent Penn
South lot **1 = 393,100 square feet**. DOF transaction **96947**, effective
September 14, 2022, apportioned old lot 1 into **1+20**, citing an Alexander
Tsukerman survey dated September 13, 2022. The later parcel record gives lot
**20 = 40,261 square feet**. The saved [2026–27 DOF assessment](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_followup_2026-09-20_four_large/eighth_avenue_dof_2027.html)
identifies **341 Eighth Avenue**, block 751 lot 20, **40,261 square feet**,
**seven stories**, owner Penn South's Mutual Redevelopment Houses. The address
variant is material to note, but the saved DOB filing is indexed to the same
lot 20 and seven-story 335 Eighth Avenue project.

Penn South's first-party [2023 annual report](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_followup_2026-09-20_four_large/eighth_avenue_penn_south_2023.pdf),
physical pages **5 and 14**, says it replaced the all-commercial building at
the northwest Eighth Avenue/West 26th Street corner and entered a ground
lease for its **335 Eighth Avenue parcel**. Its [2024 audited financial
statement](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_followup_2026-09-20_four_large/eighth_avenue_penn_south_2024.pdf),
physical page **48**, dates that ground lease to **December 2022**. The
developer's first-party project page identifies the new seven-story building
and residential use but uses a later unit estimate; retain the frozen DOB/HDB
**188**. These sources establish a distinct project parcel on the Penn South
campus, not a campus-wide development.

The current lot 20 area **40,261** is a well-bounded candidate and the huge
393,100-square-foot old campus value is inappropriate. The lease itself and
the cited Tsukerman survey have not been obtained, so there is no direct
record tying every foot of lot 20 to the leased site or establishing whether
common Penn South land serves the filing. Also, old 21v1 lot 1's numeric
393,100 and 23v3 successor attributes **350,782 + 40,261 = 391,043** differ
by **2,057 square feet**, while their source polygons conserve area. This
signals attribute revision rather than evidence for adding campus land.
Confirm the ground-lease exhibit or DOB ZD1 before final legal ground
certification.

**Supervisor adjudication:** Keep **40,261 square feet pending** the lease
exhibit or equivalent project-specific site plan. The old campus-wide area
is not this building's ground.

## `historical__M00580473`: 509 West 48th Street

The frozen filing has **158 units**, filed August 31, 2021, Manhattan block
1077 lot 29. Production carries the old 20v8 MapPLUTO recorded attribute
**57,027 square feet**, although that archived polygon measures only about
**48,119 square feet**. NYC HPD's archived [2022 certified EAS](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_followup_2026-09-20_four_large/west48_city_eas_2022.pdf)
explicitly distinguishes the **47,027-square-foot project area** from its
western **21,923-square-foot housing development site** (physical pages
**19, 32 and 40**). Figure 1-1, physical page **21**, visually divides the
western development site from the eastern open-space parcel; it was checked
against the text. Its early **163-unit** planning estimate is not the frozen
filing count. The [October 12, 2022 Council project summary](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_followup_2026-09-20_four_large/west48_council_2022.pdf),
physical page **33**, identifies tentative **new lot 29** as the housing
*disposition area* and **new lot 35** as the *easement area*, with **157
dwellings plus one superintendent unit**. The latter totals the 158-unit
administrative count, but remains supporting comparison evidence.

NYC Water Board's November 6, 2023 published resolution (URL in source
register; direct PDF retrieval returned 403), physical PDF pages **8–10**,
recites a DOF-approved May 17, 2021 tentative split of **47,027** into new
**lot 29 = 21,923** and retained **lot 35 = 25,104**. It places the eight-story
housing project on the western lot 29, while DEP retains lot 35 for open
space and infrastructure. The housing gets a **light/air easement** across
part of lot 35 and up to **39,646 square feet of development rights** from it;
these interests do not transfer lot 35 ground to the housing site. The saved
[City Record disposition notice](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_followup_2026-09-20_four_large/west48_city_record_2023.html)
likewise separates the housing disposition from the open-space rights.
DOF transaction **103297**, effective December 13, 2023, implements lot 29
to **29+35**, citing Timothy O Connor's December 6, 2023 survey and the 2021
deed. The saved 2026–27 DOF assessments give [housing lot 29](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_followup_2026-09-20_four_large/west48_housing_dof_2027.html)
**21,924 square feet** at 509 West 48th Street, eight stories, and [DEP's
retained lot 35](../../download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_followup_2026-09-20_four_large/west48_retained_dof_2027.html)
**25,104 square feet**. The one-square-foot 21,923/21,924 difference is between
the EAS/Water Board figure and the later assessment field. The underlying
survey sheet cited by the Water Board was not directly inspected.

The older **57,027** MapPLUTO attribute is **10,000 square feet above** both
the EAS and Water Board's **47,027** whole-parcel figure. Its mapped polygon
is far closer to 47,027, and the two later DOF lots sum to **47,028**, within
one foot of that official whole-parcel figure. These observations point to an
incorrect or stale earlier numeric lot-area attribute, not another 10,000
square feet of housing ground. **21,923 in the EAS / 21,924 later DOF** is a
strong housing ground assignment for supervisor adjudication; the larger
project, zoning, air-rights, and open-space areas must remain distinct.

**Supervisor adjudication:** Accept successor DOF lot 29's **21,924 square
feet** as this filing's physical ground, preserving the one-square-foot
difference from the 2022 EAS.

The outstanding original instruments are the cited 2021/2023 survey sheets,
335 Eighth Avenue ground-lease exhibit, and 1440 Amsterdam transfer deed
diagrams. None is needed to recognize that the old campus and larger project
envelopes are inappropriate as single-building ground, but they would make
the exact legal boundaries fully auditable.
