The original downloaded records and a file-by-file URL and SHA-256 manifest are in `tasks/audits/download_parent_review_documents/code/site_research_batch10_2026-09-20_north_brooklyn/`. ## Eagle/West, `historical__321387021`

The official DOB BIS doc 01 rows for jobs `321387021` (1 Eagle, 335 units), `321387076` (27 Eagle, 108 units), and `321387147` (227 West, 302 units) all give Brooklyn block 2472 **lot 21**, owner BOP Greenpoint D LLC, and filing dates March 15–26, 2019. These sum to 745 units. Independently, the NYC Water Board's May 10, 2021 [meeting packet](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/nycwaterboard/downloads/pdf/minutes_and_resolutions/wb-meeting-materials-05-10-21.pdf), PDF p. 31, labels the waterfront **Site D** at Eagle/West “745 units (225 aff units), under construction.” That agency plan separates Site D from the adjacent E3, F, G, and H sites. It supplies a project crosswalk, not surveyed boundaries.

The [March 4, 2019 DOF tax map](https://propertyinformationportal.nyc.gov/pdf/home/index/map_library/30247220190304110524), the map effective when the three jobs were first filed, shows lot 21 after the December 20, 2018 merger/reapportionment into lots 2, 3, and 21. The older 18v1_1 lot areas of 64,068 (2) and 58,823 (21), totaling 122,891 square feet, precede that reapportionment; their union is not the three jobs' filing-era lot 21. The 2019–20 DOF [final assessment roll for lot 21](https://a836-pts-access.nyc.gov/care/datalets/datalet.aspx?LMparent=20&UseSearch=no&jur=65&mode=asmt_fin_2020&pin=3024720021&taxyr=2026) reports an administrative **160,511 square feet**, unchanged in the 2020–21 roll, whereas the filing-era map polygon measured in the frozen geometry audit is about **103,391 square feet**. The roll rises to 225,115 in 2021–22 and falls to 93,507 after the 2022 split, so its area field is a conflicting administrative measure, not a reliable substitute for the mapped cohort boundary. The same 2019–20 roll reports separate lots 2=9,565, 3=7,308, and 10=3,153 square feet.

The April 2, 2019 ACRIS [zoning certificate](https://a836-acris.nyc.gov/DS/DocumentSearch/DocumentDetail?doc_id=2019050900102007), PDF pp. 3–4, identifies BOP Greenpoint D as fee owner of lots 2, 10, 3, and 21 and refers to Subparcel D agreement CRFN `2018000185733`. Its pp. 5–6 describe lot 21 separately from lots 2, 3, and 10, with present lot 3 excepted from the lot 21 description; p. 8 draws the separate lots. The certificate covers a larger zoning tract and cannot by itself assign all of that land to the 745 units. The ACRIS master index identifies the cited Subparcel D agreement as document `2018053100208011`, dated May 23 and recorded June 5, 2018. Its original image is still needed for any express development-site schedule or shared-waterfront-land treatment.

A later NYC City Planning Commission [Eagle Street demapping decision](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/planning/download/pdf/about/cpc/250033.pdf), C 250033 MMK, PDF pp. 1–2 and 4, clarifies the role of one nearby access strip. The mapped but unbuilt Eagle Street west of West Street is **privately owned by BOP Greenpoint D**, opened to the public in 2023 as a waterfront upland connection, and carries private sewer infrastructure maintained by the owner. The Brooklyn Borough President appendix describes the approximately 537-by-60-foot strip as both a pedestrian connection and a **vehicular access route for adjacent development sites**. This retrospective 2025 source shows that private title and public access overlap, but does not allocate the strip exclusively to the 745-unit towers.

**Proposed audit treatment:** the three DOB/HDB filings belong to the 745-unit Site D development and all carry cohort lot 21, while the old 122,891-square-foot lot 2+21 sum is excluded. Quantifying Site D's land requires the Subparcel D schedule or DOB site/zoning plans to resolve whether the mapped cohort lot-21 polygon, dedicated esplanade, and adjacent lots 2/3/10 comprise one economic ground. The conflicting 160,511-square-foot assessment entry cannot clear that question. The 2022 successor lot 21 and subsequent split are later facts, not a substitute for the March 2019 ground.

A targeted search for an OER project fact sheet like Dupont's used the exact strings `"BOP Greenpoint D" "remedial"`, `"Greenpoint Landing Site D" "remedial"` and `"cleanup"`, `"1 Eagle Street" "VCP" Greenpoint`, `"227 West Street" "VCP" Greenpoint`, and NYC Community Board 1 PDF-restricted variants on September 20. No Site D OER filing-era boundary sheet was identified. Results instead concerned Greenpoint H3 or the later Eagle Street demapping. This negative search does not establish that no such record exists; the specific missing fact is whether Subparcel D assigns any of lots 2/3/10 or the access/park land exclusively to the 745-unit development.

## 16 Dupont, `historical__B00604543`

The official DOB NOW row `B00604543-I1` gives 16 Dupont Street, Brooklyn block 2494 lot 6, owner Greenpoint Landing Lot 6 LLC, filing October 26, 2021, and 381 proposed dwelling units. The older 21v1 areas of 12,194 for lot 6 and 11,890 for lot 10 (24,084 combined) cannot simply be assigned to that filing.

The NYC Water Board [May 10, 2021 packet](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/nycwaterboard/downloads/pdf/minutes_and_resolutions/wb-meeting-materials-05-10-21.pdf), PDF pp. 23 and 26–27, records that old city lot 6 was approximately **11,714 square feet**: roughly **2,703** square feet had been transferred to DOT for streetbed, leaving a **9,011-square-foot** parcel to be conveyed. The same packet and [City Record hearing notice](https://a856-cityrecord.nyc.gov/RequestDetail/20210507011) explain that this disposition and related air rights also supported a separate 374-unit affordable development on block 2472 lot 70; those rights and units do not prove the 381-unit Dupont building's footprint. The Water Board packet's p. 31 plan labels the lot 6 conveyance location and separate Site D.

The June 24, 2021 recorded [development-rights agreement](https://a836-acris.nyc.gov/DS/DocumentSearch/DocumentDetail?doc_id=2021070601644010) supplies sharper physical bounds: PDF p. 39, Exhibit C, names “Lot 6 Land” as **9,011 square feet** (approximately 100.105 × 90.02 feet); pp. 45–46 describe adjoining lot 10 as **11,890 square feet**; p. 52 lists the city-retained street portion as 2,703 square feet. Agreement pp. 8–9 distinguish conveyance of the 9,011-square-foot parcel from Greenpoint Landing Associates' retention of lot 10. Earlier, the NYC City Planning Commission's 2013 [C 140019 HAK decision](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/planning/download/pdf/about/cpc/140019.pdf), PDF pp. 11–12, contemplated a 40-story tower partly on then-city lot 6 and partly on an adjacent Southern GLA parcel. This was an earlier proposal, so did not alone prove the actual filing's footprint.

The decisive site crosswalk is NYC Office of Environmental Remediation's January 2022 fact sheet, preserved within Brooklyn Community Board 1's [February 8 meeting packet](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/brooklyncb1/downloads/pdf/meeting-minutes/Combine_Public_Hearing_and_Board_Meeting_Minutes_2-8-22.pdf), **PDF pp. 159–160**. Page 159 identifies the applicant as **Greenpoint Landing Lot 6 LLC**, the address as **16 Dupont Street**, the site as **block 2494, lots 6 and 10**, and the site area as **20,900 square feet**. It describes a proposed **40-story mixed-use residential and commercial building** with partial cellar and landscaped courtyard on that site, closely matching the later 41-story, 381-unit DOB filing at the same address and owner. Page 160 outlines the site in red on an aerial view, excluding Dupont Street and adjoining properties. This is an explicit building-site allocation, not merely a zoning lot or air-rights schedule. The [November 10, 2021 DOF map](https://propertyinformationportal.nyc.gov/pdf/home/index/map_library/30249420211110150453) shows the two parcels separately near filing, while the [February 14, 2022 successor map](https://propertyinformationportal.nyc.gov/pdf/home/index/map_library/30249420220214140934) places their combined Dupont frontage in a single lot 6. The current administrative lot-6 area is **20,890 square feet**, 10 less than OER's round 20,900 and 11 less than the agreement's two component figures. Neither the 2,703-square-foot roadbed nor offsite development-rights donor land is building ground.

**Root adjudication:** the OER fact sheet establishes former lot 6 + lot 10 as the physical development site for the observed 16 Dupont filing. The root audit accepted the recorded legal component sum, **9,011 + 11,890 = 20,901 square feet**. OER's 20,900 is a rounded site measure; successor DOF lot 6 is **20,890 square feet**, 11 less than the accepted legal sum. The extant production value 12,194 comes from a different, older lot-6 vintage and includes an area subsequently reassigned to streetbed while missing adjoining lot 10. The attempted full 118-page ACRIS export of later document `2021112201527002` returned HTTP 429 on September 20; its unviewed pages are no longer needed to establish the gross two-lot building site. Eagle remains unadjudicated pending its Subparcel D ground allocation.
