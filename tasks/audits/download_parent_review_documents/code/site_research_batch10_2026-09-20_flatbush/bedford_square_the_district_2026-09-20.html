The District

Four Buildings, One District

Bedford Square unites four buildings into a single, cohesive destination. Thoughtfully designed residences, inviting courtyards, and modern conveniences come together to create a lifestyle that feels distinctly Brooklyn, yet entirely its own.

More Than a Residence

At Bedford Square, home extends beyond your front door. Shared lounges, fitness centers, and landscaped outdoor spaces are designed to spark connection and foster a sense of belonging—transforming daily life into an experience of community and comfort.

Bedford Square
Flatbush’s New Urban Sanctuary

Bedford Square offers thoughtfully designed studio, one and two bedroom residences with eco-friendly finishes, smart home amenities, and open concept layouts, all tailored for modern comfort and style.

Rooted in the cultural energy of Flatbush yet designed as a retreat from it all, Bedford Square offers the best of both worlds. Just steps from local transit, and vibrant dining, residents can enjoy the pulse of the city while returning home to a serene community defined by greenery, thoughtful amenities, and modern comfort.
