\section*{September 10, 2026: Data readiness and task review}
\textit{Agent-drafted review of the current administrative-count baseline.}

The selected counts follow the agreed Housing Database priority with DOB fallback. Outside documentary counts do not replace them. Uniqueness and refiling-date checks pass, and parent totals match their retained constituents. These checks do not establish that all economic parents have been identified. Structural estimation should wait for the remaining membership review and an explicit treatment of incomplete follow-up.

\textbf{Remaining uncertainty.} The nearby-filing screen contains 1,206 parent--neighbor rows, including reciprocal comparisons; 1,086 lack individual adjudication. Among these, 75 rows have the same recorded owner. Deduplicating by sample and the unordered pair of filing identifiers leaves 48 filing pairs: 35 historical and 13 post-policy. Twenty-six were filed within 30 days. These are candidates, not confirmed missing links or distinct development opportunities. All 48 belong to the broader screen outside the currently reviewed multi-building parents. Examples include 2455 Third Avenue and 227 East 134th Street, and 240 and 232 Fourth Avenue. Shared ownership and proximity alone do not justify automatic acceptance.

The casebook covers 66 multi-building A/B parents with at least 50 units and complete composition features. The full A/B sample at that size contains 86 multi-building parents; 20 are outside that casebook because their composition features are incomplete. Review of the feature-complete sample cannot be described as verification of every parent. Across all A/B parents of at least 50 units, features are incomplete for 2 of 562 historical parents and 24 of 333 post-policy parents.

Of those 333 post-policy parents, 251 lack a full 365-day follow-up window at the July 8, 2026 source cutoff. Later filings can change their component count and total. This is observed censoring, not an arithmetic mistake. Restricting follow-up or explicitly modeling it remains a research decision. Common sponsorship also does not establish that buildings are distinct legal wage-assessment units. Only three post-policy multi-building parents in this size/sample group have the panel's verified separate-unit status. The economic-parent definition remains distinct from that legal classification.

\textbf{Confirmed corrections.} The historical membership column labeled DOB units had repeated Housing Database units. It now matches the saved DOB source directly and leaves unavailable records missing. In the full membership file, DOB counts are available for 1,092 of 5,918 historical rows and all 2,136 post-policy rows. Parent DOB totals remain missing when component coverage is incomplete. Comparing saved datasets before and after the correction establishes that the entire selected parent panel and all accepted link rows are identical; only the five DOB-comparison columns in membership change. Reports now expose the historical coverage gap. An older symmetric-parent audit also pointed to three manual-decision files that had moved. Its inputs now link to the committed manual source task, and it rebuilds successfully.

\textbf{Task structure.} There are 29 main task Makefiles and 25 audit task Makefiles, excluding shared infrastructure. Main inputs have no broken symlinks, and main Makefiles do not depend on audit paths. The manual source task is intentional and should remain. The clearest consolidation candidates are Housing Database loading with staging, and exposure-information assembly with exposure classification. The older parent-distribution task is another candidate to move into audits after preserving useful historical figures and updating its consumers. These are recommendations, not changes made in this review; implementing all three would reduce the main inventory from 29 to 26. A smaller folder count alone does not justify combining distinct source vintages or geographic linkage steps.

The full build also exposed stale descriptive exhibits from before the recent data corrections. Their saved summary still reported ten exact 99+99 parents and one exact 99+99+99 parent, whereas the unchanged current data contain nine and two. The exhibits and their reports now match the current panel. The refreshed descriptive bootstrap completes 492 of 499 requested replications; seven calibration failures remain recorded. These failures should be considered when using its existing intervals and are separate from the planned structural model.

One Make limitation remains: companion recovery recursively requests \texttt{all}. Actual builds recover files, but a dry run cannot perform the proposed rebuild and can recurse indefinitely. A bounded disposable reproduction confirmed this behavior; the root dry run also entered recursion and was stopped. This should be repaired and tested on GNU Make 3.81 before declaring the workflow fully clean. It does not establish that a successfully rebuilt dataset is wrong. This review does not certify every exploratory audit as runnable; it repairs the directly affected older audit and checks the main empirical build.

The existing descriptive reweighting/bootstrap is refreshed; no new joint organization-and-size model is estimated. The parent-link audit supplies the casebook and neighborhood screen. Findings are recorded here rather than adding another task or status system.
