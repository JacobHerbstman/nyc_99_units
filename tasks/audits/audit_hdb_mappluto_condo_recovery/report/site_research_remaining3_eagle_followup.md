# Eagle Site D boundary follow-up

## Proposed adjudication

Use **106,018 square feet** as the development ground for `historical__321387021`, with block 2472 lot 21 as the filing-era legal boundary. This is the printed area in the recorded confirmatory deed's Langan survey description, and it is tied to the observed development through official HPD and DOB records. Exclude separately mapped lots 2, 3, and 10 and the block 2494 strip. Do not use the broad 2018 Parcel D ownership envelope, the lagged 160,511-square-foot assessment entry, or the later 93,507-square-foot successor value.

For a two-row historical allocation, the same evidence supports **54,503 square feet from old lot 2** and **51,515 square feet from old lot 21**. Those values sum to 106,018.

## Exact documentary chain

The May 23, 2018 Subparcel D agreement is broad program evidence, not the observed phase boundary. Its PDF pages 8–10 define Parcel D Land as old block 2472 lots 2, 10, and 21, block 2494 lot 5, and a future portion of block 2502 lot 1. Page 10 contemplates one or more Parcel D buildings and distinguishes the building and public-access obligations on lot 21 from a separate building and public-access obligation on lot 2. Pages 31–32 cap Parcel D building lot coverage at 73,386 square feet and development at 992 units absent later rights changes; that lot-coverage ceiling is not a land-area measure. Pages 134–137 provide the broad component descriptions. The agreement therefore cannot be substituted for the actual three-building ground.

The DOF tax map effective March 4, 2019, immediately before the March 15 and March 26 DOB filings, shows the post-reapportionment lots 2, 3, and 21 separately. The official MapPLUTO 19v1 polygon for lot 21 has `Shape_Area = 103,390.867` square feet. The accompanying administrative `LotArea = 160,511` was stale: the polygon and map do not contain a 160,511-square-foot parcel.

The later legal instrument resolves the area discrepancy. The confirmatory deed dated January 12, 2022 and recorded February 9, 2022 (`2022012501219001`, CRFN `2022000060402`) states on pages 2–3 that its premises are a portion of the two May 2018 conveyances and that its purpose is to confirm the present metes and bounds of tax lot 21. Exhibit A on pages 6–7 provides the full boundary and states **106,018 square feet or 2.434 acres**, based on Langan's Final Survey FS101 dated October 5, 2018. The survey date predates the three DOB filings, even though the confirmatory deed was recorded later.

The HPD amendments then connect that lot to the observed housing. `2021073000417011` pages 2 and 6 define the premises as block 2472 lot 21; page 9 identifies 221 West Street and 302 total units. `2021073000417012` pages 2 and 6 define the same premises; page 9 identifies 15 Eagle Street and 108 total units. The historical HPD street numbers differ from the DOB filing addresses 227 West Street and 27 Eagle Street, but the owner, lot, and exact unit counts match. The DOB extract places jobs `321387147` (302 units), `321387076` (108 units), and `321387021` (335 units) on block 2472 lot 21 under BOP Greenpoint D LLC. Together they total 745 units. HPD's instruments directly cover 410 units; DOB supplies the same-lot link for the 335-unit 1 Eagle Street building.

## Reapportionment crosswalk

The recorded 106,018-square-foot area also reconstructs exactly from the official old and retained lot values:

| component | calculation | development allocation |
|---|---:|---:|
| old lot 2 | 64,068 − retained lot 2 of 9,565 | 54,503 |
| old lot 21 | 58,823 − retained lot 3 of 7,308 | 51,515 |
| total | 54,503 + 51,515 | **106,018** |

The official polygon overlay independently confirms membership. New lot 3 overlaps old lot 21 by 6,833.123 of 6,833.136 GIS square feet. New lot 2 overlaps old lot 2 by 9,611.700 of 9,625.594 GIS square feet; the remaining 13.879 square feet falls across the old boundary because of ordinary polygon tolerance. Subtracting the retained polygon areas from the two old polygons produces 103,390.7 square feet, essentially identical to the 103,390.9-square-foot 19v1 lot-21 polygon. The legal-survey area controls over this GIS measurement.

The filing-era apportionment actions do not imply any extra parcel. Transaction 83289 on December 20, 2018 is explicitly `Step one of apportionment`: DOF marks old lot 2 dropped and lot 21 affected. Transaction 83294 later that day marks lots 2 and 3 new and lot 21 affected, citing the October 5, 2018 survey. The 83289 merge is therefore the temporary first step needed to draw the final three parcels, not evidence that the observed development requires land outside the two old lots.

The three transactions identified by the audit's unusual-change field were reviewed separately. Transaction 84181 on March 1, 2019 affects lots 21 and 3 and says only `Correction to tax lot 21 and 3 lot face.` Transaction 86750 on October 3, 2019 affects lots 2 and 21 and says `paper street removal`. Transaction 87034 on October 15, 2019 affects the same two lots and says `paper street correction`. The official maps effective July 25, October 4, and October 15, 2019, plus the map effective April 22, 2020, retain the same lot-21 outer perimeter. Their changes are to the mapped Eagle Street or paper-street annotation along that perimeter.

The archived MapPLUTO geometries provide a numerical cross-check. Lot 21 in 19v1 and 20v1 is topologically identical and has the same 103,390.867-square-foot GIS area. The 19v1-to-20v5 symmetric difference is only 0.055 square foot, with a maximum boundary displacement of 0.00018 foot. These paper-street actions therefore did not add or remove development ground. Page 6 of the 2022 confirmatory deed's Exhibit A also carries forward the October 5, 2018 survey boundary: it begins 30.035 feet south of Eagle Street's northerly line extended and runs 426.74 feet along Eagle Street's centerline and the block 2502/2472 dividing line. Because that deed was executed and recorded after the 2019 actions while confirming the same surveyed premises, it independently reconciles the paper-street entries with the 106,018-square-foot boundary. This does not backdate a street-law change; it establishes that the economic ground's outer metes and bounds did not change.

The lot-21 boundary includes the phase's privately owned public-access and amenity ground. The 2018 agreement makes Parcel D Owner responsible for constructing and maintaining those public-access areas, and the confirmatory deed includes the land within the surveyed premises. This is part of the economic development ground under the existing land convention. The separately mapped lot-2 component was contemplated for a different building and is outside the observed three-job phase.

## Remaining uncertainty

Original DOB ZD1/site-plan sheets for the three 2019 BIS jobs were not publicly downloadable. NYC DOB states that older eSubmit plans require an authenticated DOB NOW records request. No inference is made from an unavailable plan. The missing sheets are no longer material to the ground boundary because the recorded survey, the filing-era map, two HPD premises schedules, and all three DOB lot identifiers converge on lot 21.

The 2.5 percent difference between the recorded 106,018-square-foot survey and the 103,390.867-square-foot MapPLUTO polygon is a measurement/source distinction, not a conflicting parcel membership claim. Later DOF area values reflect subsequent administrative updates and do not replace the filing-era surveyed boundary.
