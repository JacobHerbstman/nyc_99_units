## Sackett

In [DOB NOW](https://a810-dobnow.nyc.gov/publish/Index.html#!/), search job
`B00665570`, expand **BUILD: Job Filings**, filter Filing# to `I1`, and open
the row's view icon. The live filing is headed B00665570 / I1 and its listing
shows a June 8, 2026 modified date. Under **Zoning Information** it displays:

- Lot Area Total: 48,500.00 square feet.
- District areas: R7X 35,000; R6A 13,500 square feet.
- Dwelling units: 300 for the building and 300 for the lot.
- Tax lots: `17, 47, 41` as displayed.
- Zoning Exhibit 1: `2025000213961`; Exhibit 3: `2025000213962`.

The Documents section lists a ZD1 zoning diagram with Created On January 20,
2022, a November 10, 2025 document named “initial survay,” and a November 17,
2025 tentative-tax-lot document. The ZD1 link did not yield an inspectable
document in this session. A creation date on this list does not certify the
contents of an original 2022 version. The live I1 record can incorporate later
changes. A direct request for the displayed PW1 response returned HTTP 403;
no raw response was obtained or substituted.

[ACRIS document 2025080501331002](https://a836-acris.nyc.gov/DS/DocumentSearch/DocumentDetail?doc_id=2025080501331002),
CRFN 2025000213962, is a zoning-lot description dated August 5, 2025 and
recorded August 11, 2025. Its parcel index identifies Brooklyn block 426,
lot 17. In **View Document**, page 2 (Exhibit III) explicitly lists tentative
lots **16, 17, and 47**, formerly lot 17, owned by 242 Nevins Inc. Page 4
contains the 125-by-100-foot description of tentative lot 47 and the diagram
of the three tentative parcels. Thus the declaration's lot list differs
from the live DOB text's `17, 47, 41`; preserve that discrepancy. It also
shows why removing lot 16 from a later cleanup/sale boundary need not remove
it from the documented 2025 zoning lot.

The ACRIS parcel search for older lots 17, 44, and 49 also returned
[document 2022022201337002](https://a836-acris.nyc.gov/DS/DocumentSearch/DocumentDetail?doc_id=2022022201337002),
CRFN 2022000086989, dated February 9, 2022. Its parties are the **545 Sackett**
entities and its indexed parcels are lots 1, 49, and 17. It is not direct
evidence of the 563/565 Sackett filing's footprint. Do not attach it to that
filing just because one old lot number matches.

The March 2022 NYSDEC IRM work plan, saved as `sackett_irm_2022-03-04.pdf`,
describes a 38,500-square-foot **560 Degraw** cleanup site: 35,000 of old lot
17 plus 3,500 of old lot 49 (printed page 2). Printed page 6 describes an
early, changeable 12-story development scheme. This is not an original DOB
site plan. The March 2024 ESD Existing Conditions Report's Appendix table
lists 563 Sackett / block 426 lot 17 at 48,500 square feet; that table uses
administrative information and is corroboration, not an independent survey.

## Noble/Oak

DOF transaction 260940, dated March 12, 2025, retains BBL 3025670001 and cites
a deed recorded August 8, 2024, CRFN 2024000204975, and Robert Fehringer's
October 15, 2024 survey. The map is effective March 14, 2025. The transaction
does not identify a newly merged companion tax lot.

[ACRIS document 2024080500318001](https://a836-acris.nyc.gov/DS/DocumentSearch/DocumentDetail?doc_id=2024080500318001),
CRFN 2024000204975, is the July 31, 2024 deed from 57 West LLC to 2 Noble North
LLC. The parcel index identifies the entire Brooklyn block 2567 lot 1.
In **View Document**:

- Page 2 states that these are the same premises conveyed to 57 West LLC by
  a May 2, 2001 deed recorded May 30, 2001 at reel 5169, page 1555. It also
  conveys the grantor's rights in abutting streets and roads to their centers.
- Page 4, Schedule A, names the centers of Noble Street and Oak Street as
  the north and south boundaries and the historic bulkhead line on the west.
  Its detailed course description starts on the **westerly side of West
  Street**, runs 260 feet south and 679.10 feet west, and closes with 601.88
  feet east along Noble's centerline. These are not identical to the 2025
  map's 631.88/709.1-foot annotations.

[ACRIS document FT_3300007672230](https://a836-acris.nyc.gov/DS/DocumentSearch/DocumentDetail?doc_id=FT_3300007672230)
is that 2001 deed. Page 3, Schedule A (stamped reel 5169 / page 1557), bounds
the property by the **centers of Noble, West, and Oak Streets** and the
historic bulkhead line. Street-centerline rights therefore predate 485-x.
Their exact treatment in the current development site's survey still needs
to be matched; do not equate ownership of street land with building coverage
or zoning floor-area entitlement.

The 2019 NYSDEC boundary amendment, saved as
`greenpoint_boundary_2019-11-25.pdf`, removes 0.19 acres of southwestern Oak
Street because its deed coverage was unclear. Its 3.79-acre cleanup site
contains portions of block 2567 lot 1 and block 2570 lot 36; it is not the
same boundary as the 2026 two-building development site. Page 11 reproduces
a 2016 survey. Do not use that cleanup acreage as the parent's area.

The saved 2026 CB1 notice, pages 2–3, directly documents the two-building
173,834-square-foot site and tentative division into lots 1 and 10. The
current lot-1 tax polygon, the shoreline-clipped land polygon, the cleanup
site, and the building footprints each measure different boundaries.

The DOB job-number search found the current Noble property profile. Its
BUILD list did not populate, including after a reload and a search using
the profile's recorded predecessor BIN 3337623. No Noble DOB zoning fields
or site-plan contents are claimed as inspected here.

## Reopening the records

Use the document-detail links above, choose **View Document**, enter the
specified page number and press Return. ACRIS's image viewer loads scans
after the controls appear. Public metadata can also be located in NYC Open
Data's `bnx9-e6tj` (master) and `8h5j-fqxa` (parcel index) datasets, but those
metadata are not substitutes for the scanned schedules. Ordinary audit
builds do not depend on browser sessions or refresh these manual notes.
