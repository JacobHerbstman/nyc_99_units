# Reopened case 04: 1580 Story Avenue

**Question.** The inactive-inclusive 23Q4 Housing Database adds historical parent `historical__220700873` (filed 2019-06-05, 562 units) alongside `historical__X00756455` (filed 2022-06-14, 562 units). Are these alternative applications for one physical building, or two additive 562-unit phases? The adopted analysis currently counts **both** as separate, composition-eligible, weighting-eligible single-component parents. This review does not change production data.

## Finding and disposition

The two jobs are **strongly suggestive of alternative proposals for the same building**, but the reviewed official records do **not** establish that relationship to the standard needed to change parent membership or filing role. Both name 1580 Story Avenue, Bronx block 3627 lot 1 and BIN 2130344; both propose 562 apartments. The 2022 DOB NOW application specifies 19 stories and 197 feet and names Mitchell Newman (license 015609), who also appears on the later amended BIS record for the 2019 job. This does not establish the architect or design of the *original* 2019 sheets. DCP's 23Q4 active file contains only the 2022 job, whereas its inactive-inclusive file contains both. That is a source-status clue, not a documented reason for inactivity or a project cross-reference. The later DOB NOW snapshot calls `X00756455-I1` **Filing Withdrawn**, with status date 2025-10-09, which is after the 23Q4 archive. It cannot retrospectively erase the recorded 2022 proposal.

No original DOB drawing, DOB job cross-reference between **these two new-building applications**, or official phase/site schedule was obtained. The current BIS document-01 record for legacy job `220700873` has itself been amended to **1,124 proposed units** and 20 stories in 2025. Since 1,124 equals twice 562, that later field leaves a two-phase interpretation possible; it also may reflect an amendment or combined-program reporting. Its meaning cannot be read from the numeric field alone. An equal unit count and shared BIN do not decide between alternatives and phases.

**Keep the reopened other-filing conflict unresolved for both records** until the original plans or an explicit DOB relationship/phase schedule is examined. Do not mark the 2022 residential job ancillary. Do not infer a new 2019-to-2022 parent edge by extending the 365-day link window. Do not use the 2025 withdrawal to drop the archived 2022 proposal: the user-selected sample includes all recorded proposals. A later sample/filing-role decision must state whether both opportunities are intentionally retained despite the likely overlap, or whether one is a nonadditive alternative under a documented research rule. That decision is distinct from parcel-boundary clearance and from a legal wage-assessment-unit determination.

## Boundary and current analysis consequence

The **2019 physical-ground allocation remains supported** at 203,910 square feet: successor lots 1 (151,992) and 10 (51,918), counted once. The recorded October 2020 zoning declaration assigns both lots to **1580 Story Avenue** and places southern lots 35/36 at another address and owner. This is an observed address/development boundary reconstructed from later instruments; it does not backdate successor title to June 2019, allocate an individual 562-unit phase, or establish the legal wage-assessment unit. The 2019 parent's pre-existing floor area remains zero, residential FAR 2.43, broad FAR 4.8, R6, and parking use. The previous [Story boundary review](../site_research_remaining3_story.md) gives the legal descriptions and page-level checks.

The 2022 parent currently uses **151,992 square feet** of lot-1 land from its lagged MapPLUTO release. The 2020 declaration's 1580 Story address assignment includes lot 10 as well, so lot 1 alone is not demonstrated to be the complete physical ground of the 2022 design. Conversely, sharing the address does not prove that each 562-unit scheme used all 203,910 square feet. The exact 562-design boundary therefore also awaits a DOB site/zoning plan or equivalently explicit official site schedule; no area change for the 2022 parent is recommended from this screen alone.

The current `parent_opportunity_panel.parquet` has two single-component 562-unit rows, each with `composition_eligible = TRUE`, `parent_constituent_weight = 1` in the constituent panel, and `included_ab = TRUE`: 2019 at 203,910 square feet and 2022 at 151,992. Thus the analysis presently treats them as **two opportunities**, totaling 1,124 proposed units across these rows. This is a counting consequence, not evidence that 1,124 units were approved or built. The 2022 case's own parcel-map screen says “Supported by available parcel and filing checks” because the conflicting 2019 job is earlier; that screen does not adjudicate additive project identity.
