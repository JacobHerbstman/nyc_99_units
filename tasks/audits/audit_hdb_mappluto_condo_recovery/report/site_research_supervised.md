## Area convention and reproduction

The model conditions on the observed development opportunity. Documents assign
the building ground and any common land; archival parcels supply earlier land
characteristics within that footprint. A later recorded boundary can establish
that footprint without proving earlier ownership. Ground leases count as
building ground; transferred floor-area rights and nonexclusive easements over
retained neighbors do not add their entire parcels.

Run `make dof-site-review`. The existing linear audit reads the named source
files and both review tables. It validates documentary reference areas against
the named archival vintage, searches both HDB and DOB lot identifiers plus
reviewed parcel lists, and preserves all matched filing evidence. Independent
source and pending-membership flags remain visible. SaveData writes reports
as side effects. The source folders preserve raw PDFs, acquisition URLs,
reviewed pages, and checksums. No production task depends on this audit.
