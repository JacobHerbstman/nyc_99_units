\section*{2026-09-15: Citywide DOF reconstruction leaves 166 weighting-sample parents for review}
\textit{Agent-drafted research entry.}

The complete DOF pass leaves a parcel-review flag for \textbf{166 of the 877
parents currently used for weighting}: 120 of 561 historical parents and 46
of 316 post-policy parents. The other 711 pass the administrative screen.
Including the 18 otherwise eligible parents excluded by composition or
identifier requirements gives 895 reviewed parents and 180 flagged parents.
A flag identifies a question to resolve, not a confirmed error or a requirement
for manual adjudication. Passing this screen does not independently establish
the development boundary, pre-policy ownership, or the wage-assessment unit.

Among the current 877, the primary flags are 149 parents covering only part of
a lot-change transaction, five missing a candidate lot in the reference PLUTO
release, three associated with several condominium base lots, five involving
other physical changes, and four with unexplained HDB--DOB parcel disagreements.
For example, DOB names Queens block 670 lot 4 for 35-53 41 Street, while HDB
names lot 47. The downloaded DOF relationships do not establish their
equivalence. Partial transactions require a comparison of the land on each side
of the change; adding every old lot would risk including another site's land.

The general rules recover Wyckoff's old lots 19/42/51 and Third Avenue's old
lots 27/30/31/35/135, yielding 50,692 and 22,247 square feet, respectively.
No project-specific exceptions enter the scripts. Sullivan retains a flag for
proposed parcels absent from the reference map and completed lineage. Jamaica's
six condominium base lots plus lots 98/99 total 111,540 square feet of associated
land; the extent of the residential proposals within that land remains uncertain.
Among current parents that pass the screen, 257 have different candidate areas
from production: 196 historical and 61 post-policy parents. Production land
characteristics, weights, and pilot estimates remain provisional; this audit
does not replace them or change units and parent membership.

The source task retains nine citywide tables with 1,589,845 rows and 39,777
distinct header transactions. Nine ZIPs preserve 484 original API responses,
exact query URLs, and SHA-256 hashes. The R scripts check transaction keys,
construct explicit old/new parcel sets and condominium links, walk completed
changes backward by date and timestamp, and join the resulting parcels to the
existing reference vintages. New/Dropped actions under generic DOF editor
labels are usable. Partial events, missing reference parcels, and unexplained
administrative identifier differences remain visible. The application tracker
and dated map index supply references for subsequent review.
