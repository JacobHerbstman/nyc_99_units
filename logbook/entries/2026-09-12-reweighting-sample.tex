\section*{September 12, 2026: Which parents enter the reweighting sample?}
\textit{Agent-drafted research entry.}

The current reweighting sample excludes nine rental parents solely because
multiple additive filings share a building identification number (BIN): two
historical parents and seven post-period parents. These parents have complete
lot coverage. The calibration summary labels all exclusions as incomplete
features, so that label does not describe the full selection rule.

Among the adopted rental parents with at least 50 units, the canonical panel
contains 562 historical parents from 2019--2022 and 333 post-period parents from
January 2025--July 8, 2026. The composition-eligibility rule leaves 560 and 309,
respectively. The historical exclusions are the two repeated-BIN cases. The
post-period exclusions comprise seven repeated-BIN cases and 17 parents with
incomplete lot coverage. Lot coverage requires a matched parcel with positive
area for every source filing in the parent; this includes source filings whose
units are not additive.

The repeated-BIN exclusions include three post-period parents recorded as
$99+99$: 229 Shepherd Avenue/240 Highland Place, 2433 Atlantic Avenue/28 Havens
Place, and 937/951 East 108 Street. They also include the $99+99+99+99$ parent at
54/62 Sullivan Place and 73/99 Empire Boulevard. This rule therefore affects
the organizational outcomes central to the joint model. A shared BIN warrants
checking what the identifier represents and whether the retained filings
duplicate a building. Its role in sample selection remains a research question.

Recomputing the calibration from the current canonical panel reproduces the
saved weights to floating-point precision (maximum absolute difference
$2.22\times10^{-16}$). The weights sum to 309. This verifies the saved
calculation for the selected sample; adopting it for estimation also requires
resolving the sample restrictions and the timing of the matched site features.
